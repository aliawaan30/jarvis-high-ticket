# Jarvis 2.0 skills this course needs

The course is only as good as the skills behind it. Each lesson that says "run
the skill" needs one of these to exist. Status is honest: nothing here is
marked done until it has been run end to end on a real market and the output
was good enough to sell from.

Status key: NEW = does not exist. PORT = exists in the free course, needs
upgrading for custom work. LIVE = built and proven.

## Module 2, the offer

| Skill | What it takes in | What it must produce | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Underserved market research | Country, their background, a revenue range | Five markets with evidence: volume, money, pain, who is already serving them and badly | NEW |
| Custom implementation research | One market | The workflows that are broken, what gets installed to fix them, what it is worth per year | NEW |
| Offer brief | Output of either research skill | One brief: who buys, their pain, what gets installed, why they pay. Both skills land in this same format | NEW |
| Offer pressure test | The offer brief | Attacks the offer as a sceptical buyer and names what will not survive a call | NEW |
| Demo personaliser | A demo from the library plus a target market | The demo rewritten in their market's language with their numbers | NEW |

The two research skills are the centre of the whole product. One finds
opportunities for repeatable services, the other investigates custom
implementations. Both must end in the same offer brief, otherwise Module 2
falls apart in the middle.

## Build order

1. The two research skills and the offer brief. Nothing else can be recorded
   until Module 2 works, because every later module runs on its output.
2. Proposal generator and call reviewer. Highest leverage per hour of yours.
3. Enrichment and trigger finder. They decide whether outreach lands.
4. Everything else as the module gets filmed.
