# COURSE MAP, the high ticket offer

_created 2026-10-28, structure set, content blank_

## In one sentence

Take someone who can already build with AI, and turn them into someone who
finds an underserved market, builds one custom offer for it, fills a pipeline
from six channels, closes it over two calls, and delivers it either through a
developer or themselves.

## What makes it different to the free course

The free course sells a service that already exists: a website, a receptionist,
a walkthrough video. Known thing, known price, few hundred to a few thousand.

This one builds an offer that does not exist yet, for a market nobody is
serving properly, at five figures. The research is the product. Jarvis does the
research, the scoping, the proposals and the pipeline, which is why Module 1 is
the install and not an intro.

## The rule every lesson follows

Each lesson leaves them holding something: an offer brief, an account list, a
live campaign, a page, a proposal, a delivered system. If a lesson cannot name
what the member walks out with, the lesson is wrong. That line renders in a
box at the top of every page, above the first step.

## The live layer

The thing that makes this worth the price is not the pages, it is that the
pages get recorded with real members going through their own build.

- Offer strategy sessions: they research with Jarvis, you choose the buyer,
  problem, service and offer with them on the call.
- Technical scoping: a technical partner confirms how it gets built, what
  delivery costs and what can be promised.
- Call reviews and implementation sessions: members bring real calls, offers
  and builds. The useful ones get edited into the course as lesson videos.

That is why every lesson has a video slot and the caption names the member.
Record first, write the page around what actually happened.
