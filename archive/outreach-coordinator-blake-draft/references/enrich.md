# Contact research without paid data

For each reviewed account, inspect its own website, contact/team pages and other permitted relevant sources. Collect only fields needed for this campaign. An HTML page can contain outdated, placeholder or unrelated contact information; record context and date.

Return: contact ID, account ID, person/role if found, role evidence, listed business phone, published email if found, source URL, inspected date, verification method, unresolved identity questions, and separate per-channel eligibility. “Published” is not “deliverability verified.” Do not probe mailboxes to claim verification in a read-only run.

## Registry routing

Use the relevant country registry to match legal identity where useful. Inspect its actual available fields and access conditions. ABN Lookup can support entity matching; it is not a universal owner-name/phone lookup. Do not assume free ASIC data contains officeholders. Companies House officers or other registry entries may identify a legal officer without identifying the operational buyer. Provincial/state coverage and fees vary.

A sole-trader registered name may be useful evidence, but phone confirmation or an explicit company role remains valuable. Companies Office, Companies House and state/provincial registries are adapters to verify, not guaranteed uniform APIs or free datasets. If no likely buyer is found, preserve a business-line call task to ask who owns the workflow. Do not manufacture a named owner to complete the table.

Keep the result as one of: person and role supported, possible buyer needs confirmation, role only, or unknown. Optional manual LinkedIn research can help on high-fit accounts; do not automate account actions or invent profile access.

## Source and channel restrictions

Check whether collecting and reusing the contact data is permitted for the planned purpose. Automatic email collection can engage address-harvesting restrictions; “it was visible on a site” is not a universal exception. Apply the reviewed country policy to the collection method as well as to sending. Where unresolved, keep phone-first/manual discovery as an available path if eligible, and flag email for review. Website research is not a permission bypass.

Do not assume an email is usually present or quote a coverage percentage without measurement. Report counts by country and segment, and keep generic inboxes separate from named-person addresses. Lack of direct owner contact does not automatically disqualify a business with an appropriate reachable business line.
