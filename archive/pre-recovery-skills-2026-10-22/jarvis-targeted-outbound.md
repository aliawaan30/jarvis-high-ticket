# Jarvis Targeted Outbound
Draft v1. Research and preparation are available independently of sending tools.

## Campaign context
Read the approved offer: buyer, geography, problem, workflow, scope, proof, delivery requirements and exclusions. If absent, establish a labelled test offer; do not treat it as proven. Define qualifying/disqualifying criteria before collecting accounts. Identify who owns the problem, who influences implementation and who approves spend; these may differ from the owner.

## Orchestration
If research workers are available, delegate bounded roles with shared offer context and a campaign ID:
1. Account researcher: find companies and source-backed fit signals.
2. Buyer researcher: enrich the selected accounts with relevant people and public business contact routes. This depends on the account list.
3. Message researcher: examine buyer language and alternatives in parallel with account research; do not assume specific prospects have a problem.
The coordinator merges and deduplicates by company domain and parent purchasing unit, inspects decisive sources and prepares outreach only after account/contact verification. No invented workers: if delegation is unavailable, run the roles sequentially and say so. Avoid sending every worker to research the whole market.

## Account records
Maintain one record per account: ID, name, domain, location, buyer segment, observed fit signals with exact URLs/access date, inferred problem, disqualifiers, unknowns, decision-maker/name/title/source, contact channel/value/source/verification status, next action, owner, campaign stage and last update. Unknown emails and phone numbers stay unknown; no guessed addresses or personal details. Separate public company phone from direct business line. Mark research candidates separately from outreach-ready contacts. Never infer pain, budget or interest merely from company size or job titles.

Start with a small review batch. Expand only when the selection rules yield good accounts. Evidence should show why each account belongs, not irrelevant personalised trivia. A directory lists a company; it doesn't establish its current workflow or purchasing intent. Check the business website and relevant product/vendor sources where feasible. Use permitted data-provider tools; do not automate prohibited LinkedIn scraping.

## Outreach preparation
For qualified accounts return a concise hypothesis-led email, a human cold-call opener, relevant follow-ups and a discovery agenda. Use observed context honestly. Never claim an audit, client result, integration or demo exists if it doesn't. Match CTA to readiness: workflow discussion, real demo, or scoped assessment. A proposed sequence is a test design, not a proven conversion benchmark.

Choose channels based on the buyer and available lawful contact routes. Email/call/LinkedIn tasks need suitable accounts and current jurisdiction/provider checks before launch. SMS is not an automatic extension of finding a mobile number; record opt-in and provider eligibility. Keep prospecting separate from consented reminders. Drafting does not authorize sending.

## Connected operations
Before claiming automation works, inventory actual connections: research, enrichment, CRM store, mailbox/dialer, calendar, inbound events and any SMS service. Skills supply instructions, not credentials or infrastructure.
Use CRM states: candidate → verified → ready for review → approved → contacted → replied → booked → qualified → proposal → won/lost. Record suppression/opt-out independently so changing a stage cannot reactivate outreach. Stop scheduled follow-ups on reply, booking, opt-out, hard bounce or manual hold, and use event/message IDs to prevent duplicate sends. Route unclear replies to a human. Never promise terms, discounts or custom delivery automatically. No persistent response handling is live until inbound events, scheduling, credentials and stop behaviour have been tested.

## Sales-call handoff
Prepare: confirmed facts vs hypotheses, previous messages, buyer's own words, likely workflow, unanswered qualification questions, relevant demo, integration questions and a sensible next step. Discovery establishes actual volume, impact, existing process and buying authority. Technical uncertainty goes to scope review before a binding quote. The student owns the live sales conversation.

## Deliverables and assessment
Save account records, sources, outreach drafts, call brief and connector status when storage is available. Return the actual shortlist and gaps visibly, with file links. Report research completed, drafts created and live actions separately.
Judge account fit, source quality, relevant contacts, understandable problem/solution and honest unknowns. When a campaign runs, measure contactability, positive replies, qualified meetings, attendance, proposals, wins, acquisition cost and delivery margin—not scraped volume alone. Do not launch outreach, buy data or change integrations during a research-only test.
