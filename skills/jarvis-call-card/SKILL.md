# Call card

Version 1, 3 November 2026. People make the calls, never AI voice. Jarvis prepares the member so every call sounds like they did their homework, and logs what happened.

## The card (fits one phone screen)
The card always starts with the business name and its phone number as a tap to call link. A card without the number is not finished. If the business publishes no phone, do not make a call card: mark the account call allowed "no phone found" with the pages checked, and keep it on email only.
1. Business, the phone number as a tap to call link (tel:), the person's first name if public, their local time, and whether it is inside calling hours for their country (see jarvis-outreach-coordinator, country rules). Outside hours: do not show it as callable.
2. Why now: the strongest signal, one line, with its source.
3. History: which emails they have had, and any reply.
4. Opener, permission first, under 20 words. Example: "Hi Dave, it's Sam. I made you a page about the calls Coastline misses. Got a minute?"
5. Three discovery questions from the offer. The goal is a booked call one, not a sale on this call.
6. The two objections most likely for this account, each with a one line answer from approved claims.
7. The ask: a 15 minute walkthrough, offering two times.
8. The 30 second version, if they say "you've got 30 seconds": the problem in their words, what it costs, what we do, the ask.
9. Gatekeeper line: "Hi, it's [name]. I sent [owner] a note about [their signal]. Is [owner] around for a minute?" If not, ask the best time and their direct email.
10. Voicemail, under 20 seconds: name, the one detail, "I'll send it by email too", the number, slowly, twice.
11. "Just send me info": "Happy to. So I send the right thing, is [the problem] something you're dealing with now?" Then agree a time to talk.
8. If they say no: thank them, ask if they want to be left alone, log it.

Blake's cold call script, when installed as a skill or reference, is the base. The card adapts it to this account. Never replace his structure.

## Save the card
Save every card on the account in HQ (call card, call card date), replacing the older one, so the member can open it any time from the lead in Agency HQ. Mention it in one line: "Card saved on the lead."

## Call now alerts
When a lead replies with interest inside calling hours, the reply desk alerts the member with the card: "Dave at Coastline replied, call now." Outside calling hours, it goes first in the next morning brief.

## After each call
Ask one question: "How did it go?" with quick answers: no answer, left voicemail, gatekeeper, call back later, booked, not interested, do not call. Then:
1. Booked: send the Cal.com link if needed, move the deal to call booked, save booked via channel (call) and booked via signal (the signal on the card), stop every other outreach for that account.
2. Do not call or not interested with a request to stop: mark do not contact everywhere.
3. Call back: set the next action with the time they said.
4. Note anything new they said in the account.

## Never
1. Never use AI voice, autodialers or recorded messages for cold calls.
2. Never call outside the country's calling hours, or a number on a do not call list where the rules require a check.
3. Never record a call without telling the person.

## HQ (Jarvis 2.0)
Read first: Offer, Accounts, Deals, Campaigns. Write when done: Accounts: call card, call card date, last touch, touch outcome, next action. A Touch for each call with the outcome. Deals when booked.
