# Type library

Every font here is on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License (or Apache 2.0): free for commercial websites, can be self-hosted, no fee. Load them from Google Fonts or self-host them in the app. If a client wants a font not on this list, check its licence for commercial web use before suggesting it.

## Type scale (desktop, then phone)

- Hero headline: 64 to 96 px (40 to 52 px on phone), line height 1.0 to 1.08, tight tracking.
- Section heading: 36 to 56 px (30 to 36 px), line height 1.05 to 1.15.
- Body: 17 to 19 px (16 to 17 px), line height 1.55 to 1.7, 60 to 75 characters a line.
- Label: 11 to 13 px, uppercase, tracking 0.12 to 0.2 em.

## Type devices (optional, one per brand)

- **Italic emphasis:** in serif headlines, set the small linking words in italic ("Gardens *that* grow *with* you", "*The* journey *of* a bean"). Elegant and editorial; suits pairings 1, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 14. Use it on headlines only, consistently, never on body text.
- **Width contrast:** an expanded headline face over a normal text face (pairing 2). Engineered and modern.
- **All caps display:** a bold condensed headline face in capitals (pairings 3 and 10). Loud and local; keep headlines short.

## Style archetypes (one may anchor a direction)

- **Editorial monograph:** one heavy sans family, giant uppercase headlines with tight tracking, square corners, thin 1px rules instead of shadowed cards, numbered sections (this archetype only), light and dark chapters alternating, one sparing accent. Strong for agencies, consultants and B2B services.
- **Soft clinical:** a gentle serif with a clean sans, soft rounded frames, generous white space, calm motion. Med spas, dentists, wellness.
- **Warm natural:** an organic serif with italic emphasis, arches and natural textures. Gardens, food, hospitality.
- **Bold local:** condensed capitals, strong colour blocks, the phone number up front. Trades and emergency services.
- **Quiet luxury:** a refined serif, lots of space, restrained colour. Real estate, interiors, premium services.

## Avoid as default

These are fine fonts, but they are what most AI-made and template sites use, so they read as generic unless a client already uses them: Inter on its own, Roboto, Open Sans, Montserrat, Poppins, Lato, Playfair Display, Raleway.
