# Brand board format

The Brand options page shows the three directions as tabs or side by side. Each board has, in this order:

1. **Name and who it is for,** with the research finding behind it.

2. **The starter logo:** the header lockup, the stacked lockup and the mark alone, on light and on dark.

3. **A sample hero** in the direction's palette, type and voice: headline, one line, the main button. Use the client's real photo if one exists; otherwise a plain colour field with a short note naming the planned Higgsfield shot. Never a stock photo of a different business.

4. **A sample section** (for example three services), so the member sees the direction below the hero, not only the top.

5. **Palette:** six swatches with name, hex, role and contrast ratio, plus any "never use as" rule.

6. **Typography:** the pairing, a large specimen and the type scale.

7. **Voice:** tone words, three headlines, the "never say" line.

8. **Photography and film:** the art direction paragraph and the three-shot list.

9. **On a phone:** the hero at phone width with the sticky call or book button.

10. **Motion:** one line.

11. **A Pick this button** and a notes box per board, if the page can save choices; otherwise the member tells Jarvis in chat.

Keep the page private and unlinked from the client's public navigation. Remove it, or keep it behind the member's login, before the site goes live.
