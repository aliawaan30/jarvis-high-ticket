# Playbook: call to close

Use after any call one. Also when the student says "here's my call" or pastes a transcript.

1. Get the full transcript, as in "Getting the transcript" below. A summary is not enough.
2. Call one brief. The business in one paragraph. The problems in their words. What they use now and what it costs. The number they agreed and how. Buying signals and objections. Questions the student should have asked. Save to Calls, link it on the Deal.
3. Review the call. Score it against Blake's rules: did they diagnose before prescribing, did they get the number agreed out loud, did they ask more than they talked. Give one change for next time. Save to Calls.
4. Solution design. Run jarvis-solution-design. Save the link on the Deal.
5. Proposal and demo. If the price check says go, run jarvis-proposal-builder with the brief and the design. If it says shrink or not ready, stop and tell the student what to fix before call two.
6. Deal update. Stage to call done, call two date, next action, problem cost and confirmed flag.
7. Report in one short message like the "After a call" example in the brain: the number, the verdict, what is ready, what to send the client today, the objection to prepare for, and one thing to do better.

Never present an invented number. Anything not said on the call stays an open question.
Before the final report, reread each HQ record you changed and confirm it saved.

## Getting the transcript

Jarvis needs the full words of every sales call, not a summary. This skill gets them, from whatever the member uses.

### Where the transcript comes from, in this order
1. Pasted in chat: use it.
2. An uploaded recording (audio or video file): transcribe it in full with speaker turns as best you can. Tell the member if parts were unclear.
3. Base44 meeting notes, if the member uses the Base44 notetaker: open the notes for that meeting and use the full transcript. If only a summary exists, say so and ask for the recording or a pasted transcript.
4. Nothing yet: ask in one line. "Paste the transcript or drop the recording here and I'll take it from there."
A summary alone is never enough. The playbook needs the client's own words and numbers.

### First time setup
Ask which they prefer, then help with that one only:
1. Base44 notetaker: connect a calendar used only for sales calls, so it never records anything else. Turn on join and record in Meetings.
2. Record and upload: record the call in Google Meet or Zoom, or with their phone, then drop the file in chat.
3. Paste: copy the transcript from Meet or Zoom and paste it.

Consent rule for every call: at the start, the member says the call is being recorded for notes. Some US states require every person to agree.

### After getting the transcript
Save the call to Calls in HQ (deal, date, type, recorded yes, everyone told yes or unknown), then continue with step 2 below.

### Never
1. Never work on meetings that are not sales calls unless the member asks.
2. Never quote private details from one client's call to another.
3. Never claim you read a transcript you could not open.
