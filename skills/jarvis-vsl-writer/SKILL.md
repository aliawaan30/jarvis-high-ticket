# Jarvis VSL Writer
Reference-grounded recovery candidate. Read the appendix "vsl source notes" below. Do not claim all seven supplied videos were fully analysed.

## First establish the decision
Use the current offer brief: buyer, problem, awareness, desired outcome, mechanism, deliverables, evidence, alternatives, price/terms and intended next action. Identify whether this is an acquisition VSL, system demonstration or already-booked prospect video. Do not write the same CTA for all three.

For a problem-aware buyer, start with the costly familiar situation and its resolution. For a solution-aware buyer, establish why this implementation fits better than current software, hiring or another provider. For a provider-comparing buyer, emphasise fit, proof, scope and the implementation decision. This is our operational use of awareness, not a claim to have completed source research into Schwartz.

## Script and storyboard
1. Open with a concise summary, usually 60 to 90 seconds: who this is for, the problem/result, how it works, available proof and the next step. Let a busy owner decide whether the rest is relevant.
2. Explain the present workflow and consequence in the owner's language. One specific example beats a long list of generic pains. Do not turn assumed loss estimates into factual accusations about the viewer.
3. Demonstrate the mechanism: real input → visible work → useful output → exception handling. Specify the exact screen, sample or recording required. Explain what stays human and how the service fits their existing business. A diagram can explain the mechanism but is not proof that it runs.
4. Explain implementation, inclusions, responsibilities and time to first useful result. Make the service concrete without making the owner learn the technical stack.
5. Address the few objections that actually change the purchase: why existing tools aren't enough for this buyer, disruption, reliability, economics, staff involvement or delivery confidence. For each: concern → accurate explanation → specific evidence/demo → remaining condition. A legitimate limitation is not a false belief to manipulate away.
6. State proposed risk reversal only if agreed in the offer. Show its important conditions clearly. Do not create an earnings promise, proof or guarantee while scripting.
7. Give one clear next step and explain what happens there. For an already-booked viewer, prepare them for the meeting; don't ask them to book again.

## Output
Give a filmable table: spoken script | screen/action | asset required. Include a short summary opening, chapter outline, filming list and approximate runtime. Use the student's natural voice, concrete examples and clear transitions. No filler to reach a duration. Keep unverified claim notes in the internal evidence appendix; mark any production placeholder unmistakably.

## Landing-page handoff  -  Blake's 31 October refinement
The compact landing page does not repeat the full written diagnosis, numbered process or offer explanation below the video. The VSL owns that explanation: recognisable pain, proposed system, demonstration, proof, relevant objections and approved offer terms. Hand the page writer only the audience callout, headline, short mechanism line and next action, plus genuine proof assets if requested. Keep detailed contractual terms separate while making material promise conditions clear. For an Offer Testing Landing Page, filming is intentionally skipped; do not require a VSL or insert a fake video. That mode belongs to `jarvis-landing-page`.

## Review
Can the buyer understand what they get, why it matters, why this approach and what happens next? Can the student demonstrate every promised function or clearly scope what will be built? Does the script answer the ad's promise and match the page/offer? If proof is absent, use an honest demo, not a manufactured customer story. Source observations, our interpretation and student-specific decisions remain distinct.

## Blake’s marketing preferences  -  confirmed 22 October 2026
For this programme’s student marketing: do not publish offer prices, deposits, retainers or dollar-valued fee waivers in ads, landing pages, VSLs or the public offer statement. Keep pricing/economics in private sales and planning documents. Lead with the buyer’s actual desired outcome (for example sold jobs and cash collected), then explain the mechanism. Intermediate metrics such as replies or appointments support diagnosis; do not automatically make them the main promise. Do not invent a revenue number to make the headline stronger.
A work-free remedy means continued agreed work without the specified management fee until the agreed result; define the target, measurement, responsibilities and costs behind the plain-language promise. Make material conditions accessible. Do not silently replace that remedy with a published price discount.
