# Real-device checklist (5 minutes, at check-in 2)

Jarvis cannot run a real browser inside Base44: it cannot measure overflow, read the browser console, test reduced motion, use a keyboard, or show you its screenshots. The site carries a built-in site check (SiteCheck) so these checks run on your own phone and computer instead. Give the member this list at check-in 2, in plain words.

1. **Run the site check on your phone.** Open the review link with `?qa` added to the end (for example `https://your-site.base44.app/?qa`). Press **Run site check**, wait while the page scrolls itself, then press **Copy report** and paste it to Jarvis.
2. **Run it on your computer too,** the same way, and paste that report.
3. **Scroll the big moment back and forth** (the transformation, the story scene or the pinned steps), fast and slow. It should never jump, flash blank or stutter.
4. **Open the menu on your phone,** tap a link, and check it closes. Tap through the tabs, lists, quiz and any slider.
5. **Send one test enquiry** through each form with your own details. Check the confirmation arrived and the alert reached the business (check the spam folder).
6. **Turn on reduced motion and look again.** iPhone: Settings, Accessibility, Motion, Reduce Motion. Android: Settings, Accessibility, Remove animations. Every section should still be complete and readable, just still.
7. **Turn your phone sideways** once and scroll the top of the page.
8. **Tell Jarvis anything that felt off,** even if you cannot say why.

Jarvis fixes what the reports and notes show, then asks you to run the site check again until it passes.

Keep the member's part to these five minutes. Developer-level checks (browser developer tools, keyboard-only navigation, throttled connections) are covered by the site check where possible; suggest them only to members who want to go further, never as a requirement.
