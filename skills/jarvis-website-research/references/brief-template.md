# Research brief template

Save as the client's website research in Jarvis's Data area. Keep each finding to one or two lines, each with its source and the date checked.

```
WEBSITE RESEARCH BRIEF
Client: [business name], [city, neighbourhood]       Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
The one action: [calls / bookings / quotes / purchases]

1. CUSTOMER: how buyers choose
- [Finding]. Source: [name, URL]
- ...

2. RULES: what the business may say and show ([state], checked [date])
- [Rule]. Source: [regulator or legal source, URL]
- ...

3. MARKET: local competitors
| Competitor | Offer and prices | Site structure | Does well | Misses |
- Local positioning: [neighbourhoods, customer types]

4. SEARCH: what people type
- [phrase or question]. Evidence: [where seen]
- ...

5. CLIENT FACTS
- Confirmed: [...]
- To confirm (shown in [brackets] until confirmed): [...]

6. DESIGN: niche design profile
- Sites studied: [names, URLs]
- Buyers expect to see: [...]
- Photo style: [...]
- Patterns that fit: [...]
- Avoid for this niche: [...]

SITE PLAN (every section has a reason)
| # | Section | Why (finding) | Content needed |

MUST NEVER APPEAR
- [banned claim or content]

CONTENT CHECKLIST
- [ ] [item], provided by [client / member / Jarvis]

SIGN-OFFS BEFORE LAUNCH
- [who approves what, and why]

OPEN QUESTIONS
- [...]
```
