# Intake question bank

Ask in one message, only what is still unknown, numbered, with a blank line between questions. Most members can answer in two minutes.

## Always (if unknown)

1. Business name, what it does, city and neighbourhood.

2. The one action the site must drive: calls, bookings, quote requests or purchases.

3. Best customers, in the client's words. ("Busy professionals in Uptown", "families with older homes".)

4. Existing brand: logo, colours, fonts, photos, or "nothing yet".

5. What the client can provide: real reviews, credentials and licence numbers, prices or "from" prices, before and after photos with written consent, team photos, recent project photos, and their Instagram or Google profile (often the best source of real photos). Real content makes a site feel premium more than any animation.

6. Two or three websites the client likes, and what they like about each.

7. Must have, and must never have.

## Add for these niches

- **Medical aesthetics, dental, health:** the supervising physician or dentist, every provider's licence type, which treatments are offered, whether IV therapy is offered, and who will approve the site content.

- **Trades and contractors:** service area (suburbs or zip codes), licences and insurance, warranties in writing, emergency or same-day service.

- **Restaurants and hospitality:** the real menu, the booking system already in use, opening hours, private events.

- **Legal and financial:** practice areas, the state bar or regulator, required disclaimers, who approves content.

- **Real estate:** brokerage, licence numbers, the listing feed or property sources.

## Shortening it

If the client record already answers a question, skip it. If the member says "you decide" on brand or style, record that and let the brand guide skill propose. Never ask more than ten questions in one message.
