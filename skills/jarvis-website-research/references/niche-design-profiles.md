# Niche design profiles

Starting points for the Design layer. Each niche has things buyers expect to see, a photo style that fits, image and layout patterns that suit it, and patterns that look wrong for it. Always research the niche's real sites each time and update the profile in the brief; these are defaults, not rules.

## Garden design and landscaping

- **Recommended approach:** Cinematic.
- **Buyers expect:** a portfolio of finished gardens, the design process, seasonal care, service areas, a consultation booking.
- **Photo style:** natural light, golden hour, texture (soil, leaves, stone), finished gardens wide and details close.
- **Fits:** full-bleed chapter breaks, arch or organic frames, tab image swaps by season, texture backgrounds, slow calm motion.
- **Avoid:** hard corporate grids, cold stock photos, fast flashy motion.

## Real estate (agents and brokerages)

- **Recommended approach:** Cinematic or photo-led.
- **Buyers and sellers expect:** property search or featured listings, listing cards with price, beds, baths and area, a map, photo galleries and virtual tours, agent profiles with licence details, a home valuation offer, sold results, neighbourhood guides.
- **Photo style:** bright, crisp, architectural, wide interiors, straight vertical lines, twilight exteriors.
- **Fits:** clean grids for listings (one of the few places an even grid is right), full-bleed property heroes, panorama bands for neighbourhoods, sticky map with a scrolling list, strong search near the top.
- **Avoid:** arch frames, moody textures, slow artistic motion, hiding prices or search behind storytelling.

## Med spa and aesthetics

- **Recommended approach:** Photo-led and calm, with gentle diagrams.
- **Patients expect:** treatments with what they do and "from" prices, provider credentials, consented before and after photos, real reviews, what to expect at the first visit, online booking.
- **Photo style:** soft, clean, calm, natural skin, the clinic interior, hands and products rather than invented faces.
- **Fits:** soft rounded frames, tab image swaps per treatment, generous white space, gentle motion, a clear treatment menu.
- **Avoid:** generated "results", dramatic dark themes, hard sales language, anything that breaks the rule pack.

## Restaurants, cafes and hospitality

- **Recommended approach:** Cinematic.
- **Guests expect:** the menu, booking, opening hours, location and parking, the room and the food, private events.
- **Photo style:** appetising close-ups, warm light, the room with atmosphere.
- **Fits:** scroll films, editorial type, overlapping food details, menu sections, endless rows of dishes or products.
- **Avoid:** menus only as PDFs, slow sites, stock food photos.

## Trades and contractors (roofing, plumbing, HVAC, electrical)

- **Recommended approach:** Photo-led with real job photos, or diagram-led.
- **Customers expect:** a phone number in the first screen, emergency or same-day service, service areas, licences and insurance, reviews, financing, a quote form.
- **Photo style:** real crews and real jobs, clear before and after (real ones), trucks and tools, honest daylight.
- **Fits:** proof heroes with key numbers, a sticky phone call bar, process steps, service area lists, strong trust strips.
- **Avoid:** overly artistic layouts that hide the phone number, slow motion, vague claims.

## Legal and professional services

- **Recommended approach:** Typography-led minimal or diagram-led editorial.
- **Clients expect:** practice areas, the people and their credentials, how the first consultation works, fees or how fees work, results only where the rules allow, required disclaimers.
- **Photo style:** real people, real offices, calm and confident, no gavels or handshake stock photos.
- **Fits:** strong typography, editorial layouts, restrained colour, clear practice area pages.
- **Avoid:** playful motion, loud colours, unverifiable result claims.

## Fitness, gyms and wellness

- **Recommended approach:** Photo-led or cinematic.
- **Members expect:** classes and timetable, coaches, pricing or a free trial, the space, real member results only with consent.
- **Photo style:** energy and movement, real members and coaches, dramatic light for gyms, calm light for yoga and wellness.
- **Fits:** bold type, video, a sticky trial or join button, schedule views.
- **Avoid:** generated bodies or results, quiet layouts for high-energy brands.

## AI agencies, software and tech services (including a member's own agency)

- **Recommended approach:** Diagram-led editorial with live demos.
- **Buyers expect:** the problem in their words (missed calls, no website, no time), the product working (a demo they can see or hear), what it costs or how pricing works, how fast it is set up, a guarantee, proof when real, and a clear booking step.
- **Media:** live product demos animated in code (phone screens, call transcripts, booking notifications, a website appearing), real screenshots in device frames, a demo call audio player, before and after websites. Generated images only for the buyer's problem moment.
- **Fits:** a split hero with the headline and a live demo, a problem section, how it works in three steps, a demo section (listen or try), pricing or packages, a guarantee, FAQ, booking.
- **Avoid:** nature or abstract cinematic films, robots and glowing brains, stock-style people in suits, futuristic blue gradients, vague "AI-powered" claims, income or earnings claims.
