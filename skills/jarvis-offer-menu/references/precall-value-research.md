# Research the cost before discovery

Use the saved niche, country and proposed service. Find current primary or credible industry sources for the inputs that matter: typical transaction value, relevant enquiry volume, conversion benchmarks, repeated task hours or wages. Open the original source, record its date, geography, sample and what it actually measured. If no defensible benchmark exists, say unknown and use an explicit scenario rather than inventing a normal value.

Prepare a short private brief:
- Problem and why the owner cares.
- A small source table: input, value/range, source/date/geography, and whether industry benchmark, assumption or buyer-confirmed.
- Low/base/high scenarios with visible arithmetic and consistent units. Scenarios show sensitivity, not a statistical forecast.
- Potential sales revenue separately from profit, actual recoverable revenue and task-time capacity.
- Questions for the owner: relevant monthly volume, current handling, genuine new enquiries, normal conversion, average sale and margin, existing follow-up, access and delivery constraints.
- One practical implication for the offer and pricing discussion, without inventing an approved guarantee or fee.
