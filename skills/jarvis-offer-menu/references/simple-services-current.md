# Simple service menu

Updated 4 November 2026 from Blake’s course review. These are optional service ideas, not proven student results.

1. AI receptionist: answers missed and after-hours calls, qualifies and books suitable requests. Any call-taking business may fit; choose one business type for the first offer. A simple setup can take 2 to 5 working days once access is ready; integrations can change that.
2. AI setter: responds to new enquiries, qualifies, books and reminds.
3. Recovery: follows up old leads or unsold quotes, hands interested buyers to sales and tracks outcomes. Roofing is the worked example.
4. B2B outbound system: researches accounts, prepares coordinated outreach and tracks replies and meetings in the client’s accounts.
5. Custom CRM: connects enquiries, quotes, jobs and tasks around the buyer’s actual workflow.
6. AI website agency: builds useful websites for businesses with no effective site; demonstration and agreed scope are the proof.
7. Content engine: planning, supplied footage, AI-assisted editing, human review and delivery.
8. Niche AI automation agency: free audit as one acquisition route, then a specific paid implementation after diagnosis.

Explain what the business gets in two sentences before discussing the technology. Match the service to a buyer problem, then create six offer lines. Do not make students research all eight. Existing heavy advertising is a useful investigation signal, not proof of profit or an underserved market. Do not repeat unsupported largest-advertiser claims. Price, client count and delivery costs determine whether an agency can reach a revenue target.

For the simplest path: start with a niche the member knows, investigate its problems, match a suitable service, understand the workflow, save the six-line offer, demonstrate/check delivery, build funnel and connect CRM/calendar, test one complete enquiry, then start the chosen acquisition route. For audit-led work, define the implementation after the discovery call.

Generic one-job automation examples belong under the custom agency route, where diagnosis establishes the paid scope. Use agency-examples-2026-11-04.json before presenting an example as a live agency offer. For full custom CRMs, recommend developer-led fulfilment and technical scoping before a quote.
