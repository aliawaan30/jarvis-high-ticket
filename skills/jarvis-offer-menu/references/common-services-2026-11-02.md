## Other understandable services, without forcing them all into ten

- **Internal knowledge assistant:** staff ask questions about approved SOPs, products and policies. An Asmuss roadmap opportunity, not a proven beginner installation. Start with a small, maintained document set and source-linked answers; different access levels matter.
- **Document-to-system processing:** extract agreed fields from invoices, forms or orders, validate and prepare entries for staff approval. More integration support than a basic content workflow. Previous project research contains Nanonets deployments; inspect the relevant one before quoting its outcome.
- **Inbox triage and reply assistance:** sort common enquiries, route them and draft responses. Do not offer unrestricted sending from the owner's entire inbox as the first scope.
- **Automated reporting:** assemble reliable figures from agreed systems and produce a weekly summary. LeftClick markets reporting; source-data definitions matter more than an attractive AI narrative.
- **AI tools setup and staff training:** configure approved tools around real tasks and train the team. Ben AI and Morningside market training/adoption; beginners need demonstrated competence in what they teach.
- **Past-customer rebooking:** a useful recovery variant for repeat purchases or maintenance, using a different cohort from never-converted leads. Podium's Air Design case is deployment evidence.
- **Websites, custom CRMs and apps:** legitimate implementation categories. Websites remain a possible entry service; a full custom CRM is a technically supported project rather than automatically a simple build.

## Teaching judgment

For the course's performance-based booked-sales-call example, the engine is installed inside the client's own authorised domain/mailboxes, LinkedIn account, number and calendar. It can work suitable ad enquiries, the eligible old database and then targeted outbound. Cold outbound alone has per-account limits and is not the entire promised appointment engine. This is distinct from selling a bounded outbound-only installation as S05; do not silently turn every new-enquiry setter into an all-channel deployment.

The gap was broader everyday business workflows, not a shortage of obscure industries. Keep the catalogue open: choose a familiar service or bring an outside idea; use an audit to discover custom work. A small useful system can be a first paid project without being a flagship high-ticket offer. Check volume, value and delivery costs before presenting it as one. Do not inflate a five-minute task into a wage replacement or manufacture guarantees to make weak economics sound compelling.
