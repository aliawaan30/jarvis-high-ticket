# Call two presenter notes (private, never in the proposal)

Fill the brackets with this client's own words and numbers from the call brief.

## 1. Recap and collect the yeses
"Last time you told me [the problem, their words]. Is that still the picture?"
"Right now [current fix] handles it, and [person] spends about [hours] a week on it. Right?"
"We landed on this costing you around [yearly cost]. Still a fair number?"
If they lower the number, use theirs.

## 2. Walk the plan
Go through the proposed build. Tie each part to something they said.
"Does that match how you'd want it to work?"

## 3. Show the demo, only if you have one
Open it in its own tab. One job, one exception, about a minute. Then come back to the proposal.

## 4. Anchor, then the price, then silence
"If I solved your [yearly cost] problem for 10 percent of that, would that be a smart investment?"
Wait for the yes.
"I'm not charging 10 percent. It's [price], and it'll be live in [timeline]."
Stop talking. Let them speak first.

## After they answer
Yes: "Great. I'll send the agreement tonight. The [deposit] deposit secures your build slot."
Question: answer in two sentences, then "Does the plan make sense to you?"
Hesitation: "What's the part you're sitting on? The money, the timing, or whether it works for you?"
