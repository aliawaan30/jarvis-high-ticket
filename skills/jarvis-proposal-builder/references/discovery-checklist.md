# What to know before the proposal

Use this when facts are missing. Ask the student, not the client, unless they are on a call.

1. The last time this problem caused trouble, in the owner's words.
2. Who does the work now, and each step.
3. How often it happens in a set period.
4. Time per task and what that person costs.
5. Cash cost, delays or lost jobs. Keep wages already paid separate from real losses.
6. What must stay with people.
7. Which tools they use now, and whether we can connect to them.
8. Who approves the spend and who needs to be on call two.
9. What is included: users, workflows, connections, data moves, support.
10. The approved price, running costs and payment terms.

Save the source of every number and whether the owner confirmed it.
