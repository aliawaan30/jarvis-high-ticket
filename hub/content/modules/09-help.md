## Where am I? {any time}

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Where am I?
```

> see: Your module, your mission, what's done and your one next action.
> fix: If the answer doesn't match this page, tell Jarvis which module you're on.

## Update your skills {2 min}

When a module shows "Update available", there's a newer version of its skills. Updating this page does not change your Jarvis. You update Jarvis yourself, in one of two ways.

1. If you saved your update link (Module 1), send the message below.

2. Or download the module's file again and send its install message.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Update my Jarvis 2.0 skills.
```

> see: Jarvis says "Updated" and what's new, in one or two lines.
> fix: Jarvis can't fetch the update? Download the file from the module page and install it.

## Something's wrong {any time}

1. First, send Jarvis a screenshot. It knows every step and fixes most things itself.

2. Still stuck? Press "I'm stuck" on the step. The team sees exactly where you are.

3. For anything else, post in your Discord channel.

> see: A reply from Jarvis, or from the team in Discord.
> fix: Urgent, like emails sending wrongly? Tell Jarvis "pause everything", then post in Discord.
