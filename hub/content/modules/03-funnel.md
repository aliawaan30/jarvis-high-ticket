Your landing page sells the offer and books calls. Keep it simple. A full agency website is optional and comes after the funnel works.

## Install Module 3 {2 min}

[[pack:funnel]]

```prompt Send to Jarvis, with the file attached
Install Module 3, Funnels, from the attached file. Add every skill inside, replace any older versions, and don't run anything yet. When you're done, show me what you installed and where we start.
```

> see: Jarvis lists 4 skills and where to start.
> fix: Jarvis needs your approved offer first. If it says so, finish Module 2.

## Build your landing page {20 min}

Jarvis guides you through creating a Funnel app, connecting your calendar and choosing your colours.

1. Send the message below and follow Jarvis one step at a time.

2. Jarvis asks you to create a second app for your pages. It stays separate from your HUD.

3. Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar when Jarvis asks, and create a booking page for a 30 minute call.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the landing page skill to build my landing page from my approved offer. Show me the colour palettes first, and use my booking page for calls.
```

> see: A live page with your offer, a short application and a booking step.
> fix: Stuck on the calendar? Tell Jarvis which calendar you use. It walks you through it.

## Test it on your phone {5 min}

If it doesn't work on a phone, it doesn't work.

1. Open your page on your phone.

2. Fill in the application with your own details and book a call.

3. Tell Jarvis "Done".

> see: Jarvis confirms your test arrived in your HUD. Your drafted emails from Module 4 can now go out.
> fix: If nothing arrived, send Jarvis a screenshot of the page.

## Confirmation page and reminders {10 min}

Booked calls only count if people show up. The confirmation page and reminders handle that.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the confirmation page skill to build my confirmation page.
```

```prompt Then send
Use the pre-call follow up skill to set up my call reminders.
```

> see: A page after booking that tells them what to expect, and reminder emails and texts before each call.
> fix: If Jarvis asks about texts, only people who ticked the SMS box on your application get them.

## Video sales letter {15 min} optional

A short video on the landing page lifts bookings. Jarvis writes the script, you record it.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the VSL writer skill to write my video script.
```

> see: A script you can read to camera, about 3 to 5 minutes.
> fix: Not ready to film? Your page works without it.
