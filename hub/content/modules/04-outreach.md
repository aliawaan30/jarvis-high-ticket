You can start this module as soon as your offer is approved. Jarvis finds leads and drafts emails straight away. Nothing sends until your booking page works, so every reply has somewhere to book.

## Install Module 4 {2 min}

[[pack:outreach]]

```prompt Send to Jarvis, with the file attached
Install Module 4, Outreach, from the attached file. Add every skill inside, replace any older versions, and don't run anything yet. When you're done, show me what you installed and where we start.
```

> see: Jarvis lists 6 skills and where to start.
> fix: If Jarvis says your offer isn't approved yet, finish Module 2 first.

## Find your first leads {5 min}

Real businesses that can pay, have the problem now and can be reached, each with a reason to contact them and a source.

1. Send the message below with your niche and city.

2. Open your HUD pipeline as they arrive. Open one lead to see its page.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the lead hunter skill to find me 10 [type of business] in [city]. Save each one to my HUD with a real reason to contact them now and the source you found it on.
```

> see: 10 real businesses in your HUD, each with a reason and a source.
> fix: Slow? Leads keep arriving while you do the next step.

## Connect your email {5 min}

Jarvis sends from your own Gmail. Every email passes a safety check first: real addresses only, nobody who asked to stop, and a safe daily limit.

1. Send the message below and approve the Gmail connection card.

2. Check the test email arrived, and tell Jarvis which From address it shows.

3. Give Jarvis your business postal address. US law needs it at the bottom of cold emails.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the email setup skill to connect my Gmail. Walk me through it, send me a test email, and tell me anything else you need from me.
```

> see: The test email arrived in your inbox.
> fix: Can't connect? Carry on. Emails wait as drafts until you connect.

## Approve your first emails {10 min}

Jarvis writes, you decide. Nothing is sent without your yes, and nothing sends before your booking page works.

1. Send the message below.

2. Jarvis shows every draft in full, numbered.

3. Reply approve all, approve 1 and 3, edit 2 with your change, or skip 4.

4. The same drafts are on your HUD: Campaigns, Drafts for you.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the outreach coordinator skill to write first emails for my best leads. Show me every draft in full so I can approve, edit or skip each one.
```

> see: Approved emails send through the safety check. If your booking page isn't live yet, Jarvis keeps them as drafts and sends them once it is.
> fix: Approve means send, to real businesses. Not ready? Approve nothing. Your drafts wait.

## Calls, replies and texts {daily}

These run every day from now on. Use them whenever you need them.

```prompt Before a call
Use the call card skill to give me the call card for [business].
```

```prompt When replies come in
Use the reply desk skill to show me any replies.
```

```prompt Texting someone who replied
Use the SMS skill to text [name] about [what].
```

> see: One screen before each call, drafted answers to replies, and texts sent from your own phone.
> fix: Texts only go to people who replied or opted in. Jarvis will tell you if someone can't be texted.
