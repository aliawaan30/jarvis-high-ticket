Ads are optional. Outreach alone can get your first clients. Only start ads when your landing page works and you're happy to spend the budget you set. Your ad spend is your own money, so Jarvis plans a two week loss limit with you first.

## Install Module 5 {2 min}

[[pack:ads]]

```prompt Send to Jarvis, with the file attached
Install Module 5, Paid Ads, from the attached file. Add every skill inside, replace any older versions, and don't run anything yet. When you're done, show me what you installed and where we start.
```

> see: Jarvis lists 3 skills and where to start.
> fix: If Jarvis says your landing page isn't tested yet, finish Module 3 first.

## Write your ad scripts {10 min}

Short video ads, one angle each, from your approved offer.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the ad scripting skill to write my ad scripts.
```

> see: A few short scripts, each with one hook and one angle, ready to record.
> fix: Want them shorter or in your own words? Tell Jarvis in plain words.

## Make your image ads {10 min}

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the static ad creator skill to make my static ads.
```

> see: Image ads in your brand colours, sized for Meta.
> fix: Colours look off? Jarvis uses the palette from your landing page. Ask it to adjust.

## Set up your Meta campaign {30 min}

Jarvis plans the campaign and budget, guides you through Ads Manager click by click, and checks your results from screenshots.

```prompt Send to Jarvis
Use the Meta campaign setup skill to set up my Meta ads.
```

> see: A campaign optimised for completed applications, with your account spending limit set to your two week loss limit.
> fix: Ads Manager looks different? Send Jarvis a screenshot. It tells you where to click.
