Jarvis 2.0 is an upgrade to Jarvis. If you haven't set up Jarvis yet, do these steps first. You don't need to finish the free course. Do the setup, then come back here.

## Get Base44 on the right plan {5 min}

Your HUD uses backend functions, so you need the Builder plan or higher.

1. Sign up or log in at Base44.

2. Open your account settings and check the plan name. Upgrade to Builder if needed.

> see: Your plan shows Builder or higher.
> fix: Not sure which plan to pick? Ask in your Discord channel before you pay.

## Set up your Jarvis in the free classroom {30 to 60 min}

The free classroom walks you through creating your Jarvis on Base44 and the basics of how it works. Do the Jarvis setup lessons, then come back. You don't need the rest of the free course. This programme goes deeper.

[[ext:Open the free classroom|https://www.skool.com/the-ai-agency-builder-6386/classroom]]

> see: Your Jarvis greets you by name in Base44.
> fix: Stuck in the classroom setup? Press "I'm stuck" here and tell us the lesson.

## Go to Module 1 {1 min}

You're ready. Module 1 upgrades your Jarvis to Jarvis 2.0 and sets up your HUD.
