## Who the client is
- **Client:** Kestrel Developments — a resort developer.
- **Role: DEVELOPER.** They own the stock, so the deck sells *their product*. (A buyer's agent, broker or advisor is a
  MIDDLEMAN — the deck must then sell *the client* — their licence, access and service — not one product. Check this first.)
- 18 years building resort and residential projects · 600+ units delivered · 9 developments.
- **Operator:** Tidewater Hospitality runs the resort day to day (12 venues · close to 900,000 guests a year).
- No contact person named → write "your sales team", never a person's name.
- **Their CRM:** not stated → write "your CRM". (On a real deck, name the CRM they actually use, everywhere.)

## The product
- **Kestrel Bay Resort**, Bali. Status: under construction.
- **The Garden Suite** — from US$165,000 (≈ A$240,000) · 34 m² · one bedroom · garden terrace.
- **The Ocean Suite** — US$260,000 (≈ A$375,000) · 52 m² · one bedroom · balcony with daybed · ocean outlook · interconnecting options for families.
- Currency: AUD at 0.69 → say "about". Prices shown in both currencies.
- **The Owners' Club** — owners' membership: complimentary resort stays, priority bookings, member rates across Tidewater venues, invitations to events. "Valued up to US$15,000."
- **Projected returns: "on request".** Never print a yield, a return % or "high returns" — their team explains returns on the call.
- Amenities: lagoon pool, adults' pool, kids' club (kids under 12 stay free), spa, gym, yoga deck, poolside dining, beach-club access.

## DO NOT USE (unverified or conflicting)
- Completion / handover dates. Tenure or title type. Payment plans. Deposit amounts. Room counts.
- Any yield or return figure from a third-party listing. "Sold out" claims. "Best", "most secure", "guaranteed growth".
