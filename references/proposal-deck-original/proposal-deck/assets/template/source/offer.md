# The offer — Kestrel Bay Resort (worked example, fictional)

> Decide this before writing a slide; the package page (`src/s23_included.html`) and `CFG` in `src/script.js` are
> built from it. Every line price must sum exactly to its group and the total — the check is at the bottom.

## Deal shape
- Term: one quarter. Paid on signing. Ad spend **inside** the price (we pay Meta) — `CFG.price` = fee + 3 × `CFG.budgetM`.
- Pace: A$4,000 a month of ad spend at a planned A$25 per verified enquiry → 160 a month, 480 a quarter.
  (Our real average is ⟦$N⟧; the plan sits above it because a suite is a bigger decision than most of what we advertise.)
- Client's team can call: ⟦how many a day⟧ — "Recommended" on the pace dial must stay under it.
