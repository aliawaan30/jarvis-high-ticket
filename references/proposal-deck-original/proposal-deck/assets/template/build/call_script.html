Enquiry call script · Bali time is Perth time

New enquiry call

Call every new enquiry within five minutes — sooner is better. The goal is two things: send the Kestrel Bay pack while you’re on the phone, and book a 15–20 minute private video call with the sales team for tomorrow. Words in bold change per enquiry. Example buyer: Sarah.

1. Open

You

Hi Sarah, it’s [your name] from Kestrel Bay Resort — you just asked about the suites.

Buyer

Oh, hi. Yes, I did.

2. Frame the call

You

I’m giving you a quick call because your enquiry came through a minute ago, about the [Garden Suite], and you were after the prices and the details. Is that still the case?

This tells them why you’re calling. They’ll say yes.

3. Send the pack while you’re on the phone

You

Perfect. I’m sending the Kestrel Bay pack to your email right now, while we talk. It has the prices for both suites in US dollars and Australian dollars, the photos, and how the Owners’ Club works. Can you see it there?

Wait until it arrives. If they ask about returns: “Good question. Our sales team takes you through that on the private call.”

4. Book the private call

You

Now, after you’ve had a look at that, what’s the best time tomorrow for a quick private video call with our sales team? It’s 15 to 20 minutes: they’ll walk you through the suite, the resort and the Owners’ Club.

Stop talking. Wait for them to answer. Give the time in their own city.

5a. If they give you a time

You

Great, I’ll lock that in now. You’ll get a text and an email with the video link, and a reminder before the call. Speak tomorrow.

5b. If they say “not yet”

You

No worries at all. It’s just a walk-through, 15 to 20 minutes, no pressure, so you’ve seen it properly before you decide anything. And if the time doesn’t suit on the day, we’re happy to reschedule.

Then ask for the time again.

What tomorrow’s call covers

A walk-through of the suite, the resort and the Owners’ Club; questions from the pack; which suite suits them (the Garden Suite or the Ocean Suite); and how returns work, explained by the sales team.
