    The Verified Buyer Funnel, start to finishStart with the strip: one step, one reason, left to right. Then let stage one play, about 91 seconds. It slows down through the form, then moves quickly from the enquiry to the reservation. Each caption answers a question Kestrel’s sales team would ask at that moment, so read two or three aloud and let the rest play. When the first call starts, pause and open the call script: “your team calls within five minutes, sends the Kestrel Bay pack while they talk, and books the private call for tomorrow.” On the codes: “no code, no enquiry. You never pay for a fake number.” On the private call: “your team explains returns here, on the call, never in an ad.” On the last step: “every reservation is tracked back to the ad that found the buyer, so we know which ads to fund.” Phase two shows only what changes: a short Kestrel Bay film plays first, the same six questions sit under it, and the buyer books the private call themselves. “From there, it’s the same as phase one.”
