03 / The proposed system

A simple path from inbox to an approved order.

The system handles repeated transfer work. It pauses when the source or your records do not support a safe match.

01 · INTAKEReceive the order

Read from one agreed mailbox and keep the original email and attachment linked.

02 · READPrepare the fields

Suggest customer, PO, item, quantity, unit, price and delivery details.

03 · CHECKCompare to your records

Check customer/item matches, approved units, prices, addresses and duplicates.

04 · REVIEWAsk a person when unsure

Show the source and why the field needs attention. Staff correct and approve.

05 · WRITECreate the approved draft

Send only the staff-approved order to the agreed NetSuite subsidiary.

AI reads. It proposes structured fields from variable documents; it does not decide if an uncertain order is safe.
Rules validate. Customer, item and pricing records remain the source of truth. Missing or conflicting information is flagged.
Your people approve. No automatic substitution, credit decision, fulfilment release or silent posting.

06 / Build, verify and roll out

Prove each stage before moving to the next.

The six-week sequence below is a planning assumption—not a committed deadline. The clock starts after access, sample data and technical sign-off.

STAGE 1 · DISCOVERY

Confirm the job

Review redacted samples, measure current handling, choose the five formats and agree owners/rules.

STAGE 2 · CONFIGURE

Build in a test account

Connect mailbox and sandbox, map records, create checks and route exceptions.

STAGE 3 · VERIFY

Run real cases safely

Test typical orders, difficult units, missing data, duplicates, timeouts and retries.

STAGE 4 · CONTROLLED PILOT

Train and measure

Launch with staff approval, monitor the agreed cohort and review the results together.

Launch gate

No production order write until permissions, licence, data handling, duplicate protection and staff approval are demonstrated in sandbox. Agree a measurable target only after the baseline is known.

Technical detail: how the proposed workflow is kept safe
Data inMailbox message + attachment; source retained and linked.
ExtractionAI suggests fields; every value remains reviewable against the source.
ValidationDeterministic checks against approved customer, item, unit, price and address records.
Human approvalUncertain or conflicting lines stop in the review queue; staff decide.
ERP writeOnly approved, supported orders go to the agreed NetSuite subsidiary.
Failures & retriesLog the exception, retain the source and prevent a retry from creating a duplicate order.
Integration routeUse the current Celigo/NetSuite capabilities if account entitlements and approval flow support them; otherwise scope an alternative before quoting.
Pre-build checksConfirm mailbox access, sandbox permissions, licence/usage costs, data retention and an operations owner.
