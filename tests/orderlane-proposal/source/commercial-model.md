# Internal commercial model — Blake review only

These are designed test terms, not customer-approved prices, supplier quotations or market benchmarks. Do not put them into public ads, VSLs or landing pages.

## Proposed package
US$15,000 implementation, then an optional US$750/month support service after the included 30-day live measurement period. Illustrative implementation payments: 40% at agreed kickoff, 40% after sandbox acceptance, 20% at controlled launch. Final scope and terms require a delivery quote. The main proposal intentionally does not quote these unapproved fees.

Implementation estimate: 70 specialist hours × $90 = $6,300; 20 agency hours × $50 = $1,000; contingency $1,200. Total $8,500; contribution before acquisition, overhead and tax $6,500 (43.3%). These are planning assumptions, not a developer quote. If specialist time doubles, the price is no longer supported by this model.

Monthly support hypothesis: 3 specialist hours × $90 + 1 agency hour × $50 + $80 reserve = $400; $350 contribution at $750. Intended service is monitoring, incident triage and corrections to existing mappings; new customers/formats, rebuilds and 24/7 coverage require a separate scope. Stop quoting a retainer until a technical partner confirms the support obligation.

## Buyer economics
All figures below are illustrative, not a client's losses. Use net time saved after staff review and exception handling.
- Default: 3,000 supported orders/month × 4 minutes saved ÷ 60 × $35 loaded hourly cost = $7,000/month of capacity value; 200 hours/month, $84,000/year.
- Conservative: the same volume × 3 minutes saved = 150 hours/month, $5,250/month, $63,000/year.
- Low-volume: 1,500 orders × 3 minutes = 75 hours/month, $2,625/month, $31,500/year.
- Proposed guarantee floor: a 30% reduction from 7 minutes = 2.1 minutes. If this also applied to the mean, 3,000 orders would release 105 hours/month, $44,100/year. The guarantee is measured on the median; the cost model must use observed mean time. Do not assume one proves the other.

Year-one allowance: $15,000 implementation + 12 × $750 support + 12 × $600 incremental tools = $31,200. We budget 12 support months conservatively even though the first live measurement month is included. Existing platform fees are excluded only if already sunk and unchanged. The $600 incremental tools allowance is UNVERIFIED; request an account-specific quote including all IDP, ERP, connector, sandbox, storage and usage requirements.

Default gross benefit/cost 2.69×; conservative 2.02×; low-volume 1.01×. These are capacity-value ratios, not cash ROI. Net default capacity value after incremental costs $52,800/year. Payroll savings require overtime, hiring or staffing expenditure actually avoided. Sales upside is excluded.

At 3 minutes saved and $35/hour, each eligible order contributes $1.75 of modelled capacity. Break-even volume = $31,200 / 12 / $1.75 = 1,486 orders/month, rounded up. An illustrative 2× gross-value screen needs 2,972/month; this is our chosen hurdle, not a market benchmark. At $1,500/month incremental software the year-one cost becomes $42,000 and the same 2× screen needs 4,000 orders/month. A licence quote can change the recommendation.

## Risk reversal proposed for validation
Target a 30% median reduction in staff handling time on the agreed supported cohort during its first 30 live days, or continue agreed optimisation with no management fee until achieved. Confirm this target against an initial historical trial before offering it.

Baseline: 100 representative historical orders timed by staff, stratified across selected document families. Evaluation: at least 300 comparable live orders, including supported-order exceptions and manual fallback work; use the same start/stop definition, record excluded unsupported types and do not cherry-pick. Net manual handling includes review and corrections. Track rework/errors separately; an unresolved critical order-integrity failure blocks acceptance even if faster.

If insufficient volume, extend the measurement period, not claim success. If missed, existing-scope mapping/check corrections, monitoring and retesting continue without the management fee until the agreed measure passes. Original implementation fees are not refunded under this proposed remedy; client software bills continue. Client maintains access, agreed inputs, timely reviews and stable scope. The remedy applies even if the client does not buy the optional support subscription. No silent time cap, revenue guarantee or unlimited new development. Do not sign this until the technical partner confirms feasibility and the agency prices the remediation exposure.

## Route toward $20k/month
One $15,000 implementation plus seven $750 support clients = $20,250 billed revenue in a month, not $20k MRR and not profit. At the assumptions above, contribution before acquisition/overheads/tax is $6,500 + 7 × $350 = $8,950. Workload is about 91 specialist hours and 27 agency delivery hours, plus sales/admin. Seven retained customers require prior acquisition and retention. This is arithmetic, not an earnings prediction.
