Northline Services CRM proposal

Prepared for Northline Services · 5 November 2026 · Prepared by Example AI Agency

Executive overview

Northline currently tracks enquiries, quotes and jobs across separate spreadsheets. We propose one shared CRM so the team can see who owns each job and what needs to happen next.

The problem and its impact

Office staff re-enter roughly 400 records each month, taking about five minutes each. At the agreed planning rate of US$40 an hour, that is about 33 hours or US$1,333 of staff capacity each month. It is not automatically a cash saving.

Quote follow-ups depend on individual reminders. The team cannot reliably see which quotes are waiting or who should contact the customer.

Proposed build out

  • One enquiry, quote and job record, replacing the agreed operational spreadsheets.
  • Assigned owners, next actions and reminders so quotes and jobs have a visible next step.
  • A summary of open work and overdue actions, with a controlled import of up to 2,000 existing records.

The team approves quote prices and customer messages. Accounting remains in Xero; replacing it and adding inventory management are outside this scope.

Timeline

Four weeks after access and sample data: week one, approve the workflow; weeks two and three, build and test; week four, user checks, training and handover. Northline supplies a decision-maker, sample records and feedback at each review.

Costs and next step

Build: US$12,000 excluding applicable tax. Half on signing, half after agreed acceptance checks. Support: US$300 per month for monitoring and up to two hours of changes. Software allowance: up to US$100 per month, paid separately by Northline.
