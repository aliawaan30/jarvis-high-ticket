## Follow one enquiry through it

Illustrative example for the walkthrough. This is a proposed flow, not a connected demonstration.


**1. Enquiry arrives**: An agreed form or connected inbox creates or updates the customer record. A possible duplicate is flagged.

**2. Someone owns the follow-up**: The CRM assigns the enquiry and creates a task using the agreed rules.

**3. AI prepares the context**: The assistant summarises the request and drafts the next message. A staff member checks and sends it.

**4. The sale is won**: The team records the decision. The CRM creates the agreed onboarding checklist and project tasks.

**5. The work progresses**: Staff update tasks and milestones. The owner sees what is done, overdue or waiting on the client.


If information is missing, the system creates a question for a person. It does not invent an answer or make a pricing commitment.
