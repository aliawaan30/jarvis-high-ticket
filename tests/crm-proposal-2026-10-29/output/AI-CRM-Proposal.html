Follow one enquiry through it

Illustrative example for the walkthrough. This is a proposed flow, not a connected demonstration.

1. Enquiry arrivesAn agreed form or connected inbox creates or updates the customer record. A possible duplicate is flagged.
2. Someone owns the follow-upThe CRM assigns the enquiry and creates a task using the agreed rules.
3. AI prepares the contextThe assistant summarises the request and drafts the next message. A staff member checks and sends it.
4. The sale is wonThe team records the decision. The CRM creates the agreed onboarding checklist and project tasks.
5. The work progressesStaff update tasks and milestones. The owner sees what is done, overdue or waiting on the client.

If information is missing, the system creates a question for a person. It does not invent an answer or make a pricing commitment.
