ACT CRM proposal

One workspace for the job, its documents and the next step.

ACT needs a request to move from the website through quoting, signing, payment and assessment without staff repeatedly re-entering details or checking separate systems to find out what happens next.

We propose completing a CRM around that workflow. Jotform, Microsoft 365, DocuSign and Xero keep their roles. The CRM connects the handovers and gives the team a clear record of each enquiry, proposal and project.

The outcomeStaff can see the job history, who owns the next task, which documents are current and what is waiting on the client.
The first releaseA representative ACT request through proposal approval, signing, invoice and payment, followed by assessment and inspection tasks.
The decisionAgree the first-release workflow, remaining ACT inputs, acceptance tests and commercial terms.

Discussion draft based on ACT's 9 October walkthrough and retained project records. Final scope and commercial terms require agreement.

What ACT describedWhat that means for the team

The proposed change

Keep the tools that work. Make the progress of each job visible.

Current handoverProposed ACT workflow
Request email followed by manual setup.An in-scope Jotform submission creates one enquiry with its contacts, uploaded plans and a review task.
Proposal preparation across Word documents and pricing files.Staff select the approved template and fee lines, review the proposal and prepare the required forms.
Signing, invoicing and payment checked separately.Signed documents return to the record. The agreed Xero workflow records invoice and payment status separately.

Enquiries that do not convert remain available for reporting. An accepted proposal and a paid invoice remain distinct events. The BCS transition is agreed before any cutover.

Follow one ACT job through it

An illustrative walkthrough of the workflow discussed with ACT.

1. Request and reviewA customer submits a Jotform request with plans. The CRM records the enquiry and assigns the quoting review.
2. Prepare the proposalStaff check the work type, fee and template, then review the proposal and required appointment documentation.
3. Obtain signaturesThe approved package goes through DocuSign. Completed documents return to the correct record; incomplete signing remains visible.
4. Invoice and paymentThe agreed Xero invoice is created once. Assessment starts only at the payment milestone ACT approves, including any staged-payment exceptions.
5. Assessment and inspectionThe job has an owner, checklist and documents. Microsoft calendar bookings trigger agreed confirmations and reminders; unclear replies create a staff task.

The business case

Measure the repeated work the CRM is intended to remove.

AreaHow ACT can measure the change
Request entryCompare staff minutes spent entering each eligible request before and after the form connection.
Proposal preparationMeasure time to prepare, review and send a proposal using the approved templates and prices.
Signing and payment checksRecord manual follow-ups and time spent checking document, invoice and payment status.
Documents and inspectionsMeasure time finding current files and handling booking changes, reminders and exceptions.
Overall costCompare measured benefit with build, support, usage and any additional software costs. Count existing licences only where costs actually change.

No annual savings figure has been assumed. ACT and the delivery team will agree a baseline and review period. Time released is staff capacity unless an actual expense is removed.

Extra jobs and additional revenue should only enter the case when ACT confirms demand, capacity and how the system contributes.
