01 / THE PROBLEM

Extra work happens on site. The paperwork falls behind.

01

The crew completes extra work, but the instruction, hours and photos live in different places.

02

Project managers chase missing detail days later, when memories are already fading.

03

Requests arrive late or incomplete and become harder to substantiate.

02 / WHAT YOU GET

One connected process.
A clear person in control.

We connect your field records to your change-order process, so extra work becomes a complete, priced request your project manager can review and submit.

An evidence-to-request workflow: capture the instruction, link labour and materials, check required evidence, apply approved rates, and assemble the request for a project manager.

Outside this scope: No automatic entitlement decisions, contract interpretation, fabricated signatures or promise that the main contractor will pay.

The implementation

  • One project and one extra-work workflow
  • Mobile note/photo intake linked to the job
  • Evidence checklist and approved rate lookup
  • Draft request, review queue and submission log

We’ll need: Project rules, approved rates, real sample extra-work records, a field champion and the project manager responsible for submission.
