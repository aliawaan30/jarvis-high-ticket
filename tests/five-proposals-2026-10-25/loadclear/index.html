02 / WHAT YOU GET

One connected process.
A clear person in control.

We connect driver paperwork to your billing workflow, so complete deliveries become invoice-ready packets and missing documents get a clear owner.

A delivery-to-billing workflow: read uploaded documents, match the load, check customer-required paperwork, chase missing items internally, and prepare the packet for billing approval.

Outside this scope: No invented signatures, automatic accessorial approval, collection guarantee or unapproved invoice sending.

The implementation

  • One depot and one customer billing pattern
  • Document intake and load matching
  • Missing-document queue and internal reminders
  • Invoice packet preparation and billing handoff

We’ll need: TMS access, customer billing requirements, sample delivery documents, duplicate rules and a billing approver.
