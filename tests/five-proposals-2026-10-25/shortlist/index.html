02 / WHAT YOU GET

One connected process.
A clear person in control.

We connect candidate records, interview notes and your client template to prepare accurate submission packs, with recruiter approval and a clear feedback handoff.

A submission-preparation workflow: extract supported facts, map them to the role brief, flag contradictions, format the profile, and prepare a client share for recruiter approval.

Outside this scope: No automated candidate rejection or ranking, invented achievements, reference verification or sending personal data without authorised review.

The implementation

  • One recruiting team and submission format
  • CV and approved interview-note intake
  • Fact-linked brief and contradiction checks
  • Recruiter approval and client feedback record

We’ll need: Approved candidate data, the role brief, ATS access, retention/access rules and a recruiter who checks facts and permission to share.
