01 / THE PROBLEM

The visit is finished. The repair quote is not.

01

Technicians describe the fault in notes, photos and calls. The office has to piece it together.

02

Missing model numbers and uncertain parts send the quote back to the technician.

03

Repair opportunities wait while customers still need a clear price and a next step.

02 / WHAT YOU GET

One connected process.
A clear person in control.

We turn your technicians’ completed findings into clear, approval-ready repair quotes inside your existing workflow, so useful repair opportunities reach the customer sooner.

An intake and quote-preparation workflow: read the visit, match approved equipment and price-book records, flag gaps, prepare the quote, then hand it to your estimator.

Outside this scope: No AI diagnosis, guessed parts, autonomous pricing or replacement of your field-service platform.

The implementation

  • One service team and one repair category
  • Visit-note intake and equipment matching
  • Approved price-book lookup and quote draft
  • Estimator review, handoff and status tracking

We’ll need: Sample visits, a current price book, equipment records, permission to connect the existing software, and an estimator who owns approvals.
