Five possibilities.
Different levels of conviction.

My first two to investigate are HVAC repair quoting and construction extra-work capture: the commercial event is easy to understand. Freight billing has a clear operational case. Ecommerce is a more advanced integration. Recruitment is easy to explain but has the weakest custom-build differentiation.

These are source-supported workflow opportunities, not five proven high-ticket offers. Each has an existing software alternative. The commercial job is to find a buyer with a genuine gap, not attach an AI label to a feature they already own.

The demos use fictional records and show proposed behaviour. They do not call APIs, store customer data or perform business actions. No prices, testimonials, market counts or conversion claims were invented.

COMMERCIAL HVAC

Fieldquote: what we would actually sell

We turn your technicians’ completed findings into clear, approval-ready repair quotes inside your existing workflow, so useful repair opportunities reach the customer sooner.

Buyer and pain

Commercial HVAC service companies with a busy maintenance book and an office team preparing repair quotes. Decision-maker: Owner or service operations manager.

System and delivery

An intake and quote-preparation workflow: read the visit, match approved equipment and price-book records, flag gaps, prepare the quote, then hand it to your estimator.

Implementation/configuration first; custom intake only if the gap survives the native-software check.

Difficulty: Moderate · field-service integration and price-book quality

How we would acquire clients

Call service managers at regional commercial HVAC firms. Offer to trace three recent maintenance visits through to the quote. Use a short screen demo in a personal follow-up email; broad ads come after a repeated pain is confirmed.

What the evidence establishes

A named commercial HVAC customer describes same-day quoting and connected service operations. This supports the workflow’s relevance; it does not establish demand for our custom service.

FREIGHT CARRIERS

Buyer and pain

Regional trucking companies whose delivery documents delay invoicing across drivers or depots. Decision-maker: Billing manager, controller or owner.

System and delivery

Build a focused list of regional carriers, then call billing managers. Ask for their delivered-but-not-invoiced report. A personalised email with a mock packet supports the call; mass SMS is not the starting route.

What the evidence establishes

Tiger Lines describes manual document handling and connecting capture to billing. The case reports shorter delivery-to-invoice time; it is a vendor account, not proof of our proposed performance.

ECOMMERCE OPERATIONS

Buyer and pain

System and delivery

A same-price exchange workflow: identify the order, draft the support response, check policy and stock, create a held request, then release it after the required warehouse event.

Template-led integration with an experienced ERP specialist. Not a first unsupported beginner build.

Difficulty: Advanced · inventory and financial-system integration

How we would acquire clients

Find ecommerce operations leads whose companies publicly use Shopify and NetSuite. Confirm the stack and exchange volume on a discovery call; demonstrate the hold-and-release workflow to operations and finance together.
