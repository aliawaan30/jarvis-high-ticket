02 / WHAT YOU GET

One connected process.
A clear person in control.

We connect one clearly defined exchange flow across your store and back office, with a visible hold until the warehouse confirms the return and staff approve any exception.

A same-price exchange workflow: identify the order, draft the support response, check policy and stock, create a held request, then release it after the required warehouse event.

Outside this scope: No full ERP migration, autonomous refunds, price-difference accounting or tax changes. AI drafts language; deterministic rules control financial and stock actions.

The implementation

  • One store and same-price size exchanges
  • Order, policy and stock lookups
  • Warehouse-receipt hold and release logic
  • Exception queue, status sync and audit trail

We’ll need: Shopify/NetSuite sandbox access, return policy, a warehouse event owner, integration licence review and finance approval of the test cases.

What passing looks like

A duplicate event cannot create a second exchange; no replacement releases before verified receipt; unavailable stock and policy exceptions stay in a staff queue.
