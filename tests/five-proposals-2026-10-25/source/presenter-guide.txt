HOW TO PRESENT EACH PROPOSAL

1. Name the buyer and the everyday event in the headline.
2. Let the 35-second demo play once. Say: this is an illustrative workflow, not a connected implementation.
3. Replay and pause at the missing detail. Explain what the human still owns.
4. Show the included scope and ask where their existing software already handles it.
5. Replace calculator assumptions with their actual monthly volume and handling time. Released capacity is not cash savings.
6. Open the scope-call checklist. Agree sample records and technical review before any quote or guarantee.

RECOMMENDED REVIEW ORDER
Fieldquote, Changeproof, Loadclear, Exchangeflow, Shortlist. This is judgment about clarity and likely commercial relevance, not an evidence-derived performance ranking.

SCOPE
Five fictional demonstration brands. No buyer-specific discovery has occurred. The case studies belong to other providers. No custom opportunity is established as underserved or proven high-ticket demand. No integration, deployment, outreach or purchase was performed.
