*How the recovery commitment works

The target, measurement period, eligible estimate cohort and client responsibilities are agreed in writing before work begins. Recovery means verified payments collected against the agreed reopened estimates, net of refunds—not the total value of quotes or unsigned deals. This is attributed cash collected, not a claim that every payment was caused solely by the system.

If the agreed target is missed, we continue the same scoped follow-up work without a management fee until it is reached, while the agreed access, eligible pipeline and sales cooperation remain available. Third-party software and messaging costs remain payable. New services and additional locations are outside that commitment. These are proposed offer terms for this review; a live commitment requires a feasible scope and agreed target.

Before you book

Questions worth asking.

“Doesn’t my CRM already send follow-ups?”

It may. JobNimbus already supports automated messages. If your existing setup reliably follows up, handles replies and gets homeowners back to the right rep, you may not need this. The service is for teams that want that entire process configured, operated and measured.

“Will AI negotiate prices or promise a start date?”

No. It works from your approved information and routes pricing, financing, technical and scheduling commitments to the right person. Your team retains control of the actual sale.

“What if we don’t have many open estimates?”

We check the size and quality of your pipeline before recommending a programme. If the opportunity is too small, improving your existing CRM follow-up may be a better fit.

“Do you replace JobNimbus?”

No. We scope the pilot around your existing JobNimbus workflow. Access and field mapping are checked before you commit; a different CRM requires a separate scope.

“What happens after the initial programme?”

Review the actual reopened opportunities, sold jobs and payments collected. We agree any continuing service with you based on the value delivered and the ongoing work required.
