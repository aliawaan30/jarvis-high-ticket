ACT CRM proposal

Build a custom CRM for ACT’s enquiries, proposals and certification projects.

We propose building one system to reduce duplicate data entry, document chasing and the manual work needed to move a job from request to assessment.

The CRM will connect ACT’s request forms, proposals, signatures, invoicing and inspection bookings. Staff will be able to see the current documents, the job owner and the next task from one record.

Proposed build out

Build the CRM around ACT’s process, from the original request through to assessment and inspection.

What we will buildWhat changes for ACT
Request captureConnect the agreed Jotform forms. Create the enquiry, contacts, uploaded files and review task from the submission, reducing re-entry.
Proposal preparationUse ACT’s approved templates, fee schedules and required forms. Staff review the price, documents and signers before sending. AI can suggest a template; staff can change it.
Signing and invoicingSend approved packages through DocuSign and return signed files to the job. Connect Xero invoice and payment status so the team can distinguish signed work from paid work.
Project and document managementKeep the owner, tasks, checklist and current documents on the project. Label approved and superseded files so staff know which version to use.
Inspection bookings and follow-upConnect ACT’s Microsoft calendar. Link bookings, agreed reminders and replies to the job; route changes and unclear replies to staff.
Workload and reportingProvide shared views of assigned work and outstanding actions. Build the agreed reports from ACT’s tracking spreadsheet.

    Project scope

    Design, build, connect, test and hand over the agreed ACT workflow.

    Included

    • CRM for enquiries, proposals, projects, contacts, documents and assigned tasks.
    • The agreed ACT/NSW request forms, proposal templates, fee schedules and checklists.
    • Connections to ACT-owned Jotform, Microsoft 365, DocuSign, Xero and Twilio accounts.
    • Staff roles and access, including agreed limits on financial information.
    • Inspection booking workflow and reports agreed from ACT’s spreadsheet.
    • Testing, staff training, operating guide and launch handover.

    Agree before work starts

    • The exact inventory of forms, templates and reports.
    • Which historical BCS records and files need moving, and how much migration work is included.
    • Payment rules, including staged-payment exceptions.
    • Who owns data, code, accounts, hosting, backups and ongoing support.
    • The build fee, running costs and delivery dates.
    ACCEPTANCE

    ACT tests a representative job from request to inspection, with correct documents, invoice and payment status, staff access and booking changes. We correct defects against the agreed scope before launch.

    Automatic certification decisions are outside scope. Additional features or migration beyond the agreed inventory require a written scope and cost before work proceeds.
