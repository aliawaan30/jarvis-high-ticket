02 / THE OFFER

A CRM built around your business.
An assistant that can act inside it.

We connect your leads, sales conversations and client projects in one custom workspace, then give Jarvis the context and approved actions to keep work moving.

Your team can use the screens normally, or ask Jarvis to find information, prepare follow-ups, create tasks and update records. Every action has a source, an owner and a visible history.

01 · Today

Meetings, follow-ups, overdue tasks and decisions that need you. One useful starting point.

02 · Sales pipeline

03 · Customer history

Contact details, notes, approved call summaries and connected conversations in one record.

04 · Client projects

Turn a won deal into a project with its agreed scope, milestones, files, owners and blockers.

05 · Follow-up workflows

Prepare email and SMS drafts, create reminders and stop sequences when a reply or status change requires it.

06 · Jarvis chat

Ask about the business and request actions. Routine updates follow your rules; sensitive actions go for approval.

03 / WHAT CHANGES DAY TO DAY

Less “where is that?”
More “here’s what happens next.”

Before the sales call

After the deal closes

Use the approved scope to prepare onboarding, assign the project and request the information delivery needs.

Before you finish the day

Ask which deals need attention and which projects are blocked. Open the source record behind each answer.

Measure the result in your actual business.

The proposed first release

  • One business workspace with owner, sales and delivery permissions.
  • One sales pipeline and one agreed project template.
  • Contact import from one agreed source, with a deduplication review.
  • Jarvis search, summaries, task creation and approved record updates.
  • One agreed email/calendar connection; SMS included only if the provider and messaging setup are confirmed in scope.
  • Activity history, handover documentation, export requirements and team training.

What we agree before building

  • Your existing tools, integration access and the system that owns each piece of data.
  • The exact data to migrate, who can access it and how it is retained.
  • Which actions Jarvis can perform automatically and which require approval.
  • Software subscriptions, delivery milestones, ownership, support and commercial terms.

Voice calling, accounting replacement, custom mobile apps and additional integrations are separate scope decisions. Customer messaging requires connected sending tools and agreed permissions.

05 / HOW WE KNOW IT WORKS

One complete journey.
No lost handover.

Your acceptance walkthrough

  • Create a test lead and assign the next action.
  • Record a sales call and prepare a follow-up for approval.
  • Confirm a won deal and create the agreed project.
  • Add a delivery update and surface a blocker to its owner.
  • Ask Jarvis for a summary and inspect the records behind its answer.

The controls matter too.

The pilot must also show that restricted records stay restricted, uncertain answers are flagged, duplicate events do not create duplicate work, and a failed integration is visible.

Nothing goes live just because a screen looks finished. Your nominated owner signs off on the agreed workflow and its controls before wider rollout.

This proposal demonstrates the intended experience. Production connections and these acceptance checks are part of the implementation.
