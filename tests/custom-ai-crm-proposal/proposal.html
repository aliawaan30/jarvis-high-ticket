THE AI ROLE

Template suggestion only. ACT staff review and approve the work.

01

Enquiries → Proposals → Projects

Keep an enquiry visible while it’s reviewed. Convert accepted work into a project without losing the trail.

02

Tasks and responsibility

Give the quote review, project handover and next action a named owner. Give admins a view of workload across the team.

03

Documents that stay with the job

Bring in the files, identify their type, keep approved material distinct from superseded versions, and preserve manual upload.

04

Proposal preparation with review

Use ACT templates and approved fees to prepare a draft. Staff review the content, price, forms and signer details before anything goes out.

05

AI can suggest the right template

Use the request description to suggest an ACT-approved proposal template. Staff can change it. AI does not set the fee or decide whether work complies.

06

Finance and assessment handoff

Track signed acceptance, invoice and payment as separate events. Start assessment work only when the agreed payment condition is met.

07

Inspection follow-through

Work from ACT’s Microsoft calendar, record the booking, then handle confirmations, reminders and replies using ACT-approved rules.

Where the build is now

This is already a working CRM build.
The full workflow still needs proving.

A useful distinction for the team: several CRM screens and draft workflows exist; the end-to-end path through ACT’s real services has not all passed.

IN THE CURRENT BUILDReady to review
  • Project records, project navigation and staff task views
  • Proposal preparation using ACT-supplied Word templates, pricing lines, GST totals and saved revisions
  • Original government forms available to find and download
  • Appointment of Certifier property prefill and document package preparation
  • DocuSign sandbox draft and internal signing flow exercised with test documents
  • Inspection and booking screens in the CRM
  • Template suggestion logic evaluated locally; ACT’s selection rules and accepted examples still need sign-off

These are build capabilities. Each has its own review and acceptance conditions.

BEFORE ACT GO-LIVEComplete the live checks
  • Real Jotform submissions and attachments arrive once, on the right enquiry
  • ACT DocuSign account creates, sends and returns signed documents to that record
  • Xero invoice and payment states match ACT’s approved fee, tax and exception rules
  • ACT email, calendar and SMS deliver, reply and update the correct job
  • Roles, document handling, errors, retries, reporting and cutover are accepted by ACT

No client message, invoice or certificate should be sent automatically until that path is approved and tested.
