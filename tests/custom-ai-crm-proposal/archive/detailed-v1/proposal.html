What we are proposing

We propose a custom CRM that brings enquiries, sales follow-up and client delivery into one workspace shaped around how your team works. A new enquiry gets an owner and next step; an approved proposal can become a project with the agreed scope, people, documents, milestones and tasks already attached. Your team can see what needs attention without piecing the story together from inboxes, spreadsheets and chat.

The system will automate routine capture, reminders and handovers, while staff remain responsible for decisions that affect customers, money or delivery. Jarvis can find information, summarise a record, suggest an action and prepare a draft; it will show its source and ask for approval before sensitive actions. We will configure and test the complete agreed workflow, connect only the approved systems, train the team and launch through a controlled pilot.

02 · THE RECOMMENDATION

Why a custom CRM—and why this design?

One connected record for the whole relationship

The core problem is not a lack of another place to store contacts. It is the handoff between sales commitments and the work the team must deliver. This design keeps the customer, opportunity, proposal, project, tasks and documents linked, so context follows the work.

We recommend a custom build only if the discovery shows the current CRM or an affordable off-the-shelf setup cannot handle the required workflow cleanly. If the native system already solves it, use that instead of paying us to rebuild it.

WHY NOT JUST ADD ANOTHER TOOL?
Spreadsheets + inboxLow entry cost; people still reconcile status and handovers.
Standard CRMOften faster and cheaper; test its workflow and automation limits first.
Custom CRMFits a proven business-specific process; higher build and ownership responsibility.

03 · THE SYSTEM WE WILL BUILD

One workspace. Each person sees the work they need to move forward.

The proposed CRM is a role-based web application configured around the agreed process, with access controls defined and tested before launch. Exact data fields and integrations are confirmed in technical discovery.

01 / INTAKE

Enquiries & opportunities

Capture a lead’s source, contact details, need, owner, stage, value and next action. Prevent accidental duplicate contacts and make unassigned enquiries visible.

  • Agreed entry forms/import
  • Searchable customer history
  • Lead ownership and stage rules
  • Follow-up dates and overdue states
02 / SELL

Proposals & decisions

Prepare proposals from approved service, price and scope data. Keep versions and decision status tied to the opportunity.

  • Reusable proposal template(s)
  • Review and approval before sending
  • Accepted / declined / revision tracking
  • Manual or verified e-signature link
03 / DELIVER

Projects & handovers

Turn accepted work into a project with the scope, contacts, files, owner, milestones, dependencies and checklist carried over.

  • Project templates and stage gates
  • Task assignment and due dates
  • Blockers and status history
  • Client requests and completion record
04 / CONTROL

Tasks, alerts & team view

Give each person a queue of assigned work. Let managers see overdue tasks, workload, unassigned items and projects that need attention.

  • In-app notification feed
  • Optional approved email alerts
  • Workload and date filters
  • Read / unread and owner status
05 / KNOW

Files & activity history

Keep files attached to the relevant customer, proposal or project, with a clear type and status. Record material changes and actions.

  • Document type and version
  • Approved / superseded organisation
  • Search by record and filename
  • Who changed what and when
06 / ASSIST

Jarvis AI inside the workflow

Answer questions from records the user may access. Suggest next steps, summarise history and draft routine communications for review.

  • Show the source records used
  • Flag missing or conflicting context
  • Ask before sensitive changes or sends
  • Log approved AI-assisted actions
