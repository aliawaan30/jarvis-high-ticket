# ACT CRM proposal · review record

**Revision:** 29 October 2026  
**Status:** ACT-specific discussion draft; not a final priced quote or launch certification.

## Why this replaces the old page

The prior page used fictional Northstar Services pains, people and workflow. That did not reflect ACT’s discovery call, so it could not be used to sell ACT’s actual CRM. This revision uses ACT’s named workflow and plain language, and removes the template example.

## Read-through order

1. State the outcome: one request-to-assessment record, with a visible owner and next action.
2. Bring back the specific current handoff: Jotform → admin email → manual BCS re-entry → proposal/documents → signature/invoice/payment → assessment/inspection.
3. Walk one request through six interactive steps, including staff review, separate acceptance and payment states, and exception handling.
4. Explain the CRM and its service connections, then list the deliverables.
5. Distinguish what exists in the current CRM candidate from what still requires ACT live verification.
6. Cover the cost of the existing process without inventing time, savings or a dollar anchor; state the commercial inputs missing from the call record.
7. Set out controlled build, acceptance and launch stages, then ask to agree the workflow and first release.

## Evidence and wording limits

- Pain and workflow are based on the 9 October call review and action register.
- Current-build bullets are limited to retained candidate features and test evidence. They do not claim end-to-end ACT production workflows.
- ACT DocuSign account connection, Xero transaction/payment path, live form intake/attachments, Microsoft mail/calendar and SMS remain acceptance checks.
- Template recommendation is described as a suggestion with a human override. No automated fee decision or compliance determination is promised.
- Pricing, GST, recurring service costs, support, savings baseline and launch date are not in the discovery-call record. The page explicitly leaves commercial amount unfilled; it must be reconciled with the current ACT agreement before buyer approval.

## Visual choices

- One long scrolling document with no fixed navigation or marketing page chrome.
- ACT-specific process diagram and a clickable CRM record walkthrough are the main visual focus.
- High contrast navy/teal/blue palette; compact real workflow screen illustration; concise deliverable and rollout lists.
- No invented customer names, stock testimonials, fake ROI, countdown, or fictional “security team.”
- Mobile styles, keyboard-operable step controls and reduced-motion fallback included.
