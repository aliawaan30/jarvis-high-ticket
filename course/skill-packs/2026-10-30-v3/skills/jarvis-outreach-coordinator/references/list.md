# List construction

## Inputs and outputs

Input: offer version, geography, business definition, inclusions/exclusions, target account count, allowed sources and tool/spend limits.

Output per account: stable ID, business name, trading/legal-name distinction, source/provider ID, canonical domain if known, location, listed business phone if available, evidence references, source-specific use/retention restrictions, reviewed fit and next action. Missing website, phone, email or name is unknown, never invented.

## Lean workflow

- Choose a category and locality from the approved campaign. Use a connected business finder or permitted public/manual sources. Paginate only to the agreed limit; broad geographic searches can return overlapping businesses.
- Google Places can be a discovery adapter, not assumed permission to build an unrestricted permanent copy of its results. Check current applicable policies before storing/displaying/exporting fields. Place IDs and independently sourced website facts must retain distinct provenance. If the required use is unsupported, use an appropriately licensed source or manual supplied list.
- Treat billing setup, free allowances, field masks and credit budgets as connection configuration. Do not promise zero cost from a count of businesses alone. A failed request or richer field set can alter usage.
- Resolve domain variants and provider IDs. Preserve branches, parent groups and independent franchisees. Do not collapse every location on one domain into one buyer automatically.
- Check ten accounts manually for category/geographic fit before further work. Preserve rejected accounts and reasons so the next run does not reintroduce them.

Do not submit quote forms, call numbers, test email delivery or interact with a business to verify a listing in a read-only pilot. Avoid browser scraping that the source does not permit. A page that cannot be accessed is an unresolved record, not a successful inspection.

## Practical fallback

Without Places access, the member can supply a small approved list or manually identify business websites. Jarvis can organise and research that list with available tools. Label manual discovery time and do not claim it is automated.
