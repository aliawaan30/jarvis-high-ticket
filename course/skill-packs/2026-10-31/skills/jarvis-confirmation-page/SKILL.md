# Jarvis Confirmation Page
Reference-inspected recovery candidate, 22 October 2026. Read references/funnel-visual-analysis.md.

This page prepares a qualified prospect and continues the persuasion after booking. It is more than a receipt. Read the offer, entry source, actual booking status and sales objections first.

Blake confirmed 30 October: keep this skill. For confirmed bookings, lead with relevant testimonials/proof, a short rapport-building preparation video and clear actions: accept the calendar invitation and reply yes to the actual confirmation email/SMS where that reply is supported. Build familiarity with the person and call. If proof is unavailable, use a clearly labelled demonstration. Do not turn an already-confirmed calendar booking into a provisional one or invent a cancellation threat.

## Reference structure
Accurate status headline + concise instruction → dominant short preparation video → two/three visible actions → relevant proof or working demo → FAQ/sales assets addressing the concerns that can prevent attendance or purchase. Tim's numbered vertical steps and Goh's proof/FAQ depth are alternatives, not a checklist requiring every section.

Actions: check actual calendar invitation/timezone, know the meeting link, bring the one/two useful inputs, attend or reschedule. Logistics must remain accessible without watching video. Don't copy 'not confirmed' or threat of losing the place unless a real confirmation state/policy makes it true. No fake date/time/meeting links.

Write the prep video to explain what the prospect will get from the call, who should attend, what to prepare and one major objection. Use genuine relevant proof; no coaching curriculum/community sections for a student's B2B implementation offer. If testimonials are unavailable, a clearly identified demonstration can explain the solution, but cannot pretend to prove revenue.

Preserve landing-page offer, mechanism and visual identity. Keep only objections relevant to this buyer: existing software, implementation effort, required access, team changes, support, cost and scope. Reassure concretely rather than adding a defensive disclaimer list.

Deliver rendered page, prep script, FAQ clips/briefs, assets and event states. Inspect missing booking, confirmed booking, timezone, reschedule/cancellation and mobile when those tools exist; otherwise mark integrations untested. Review reference fidelity and relevance separately from working links.

## Form and booking refinement — 22 October 2026
For Blake’s funnels, ask fit questions one at a time, then collect full name, email and phone together on one final contact screen. Preserve answers on Back; validate all contact fields. Omit completion-time estimates such as “About 2 minutes”. The next page leads with “Thanks, you qualify. Let’s get you booked in” when qualification means eligibility for the discovery call, then displays the booking calendar. Do not silently claim eligibility for the actual service. If genuine exclusion rules exist, route those separately. Avoid an internal scope-review report as the prospect’s next step. Never claim an appointment exists until calendar confirmation. In previews put calendar-connection status in the review treatment, not the headline. Do not copy reference scarcity, proof or personal contact details.
