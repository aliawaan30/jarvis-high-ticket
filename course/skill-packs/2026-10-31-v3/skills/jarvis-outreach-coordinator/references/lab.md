# Lean lab and release checks

Design only. No prospect outreach authorised by this package. The lab offer is Blake's Jarvis for business owners offer, not an implementation agency offer. Keep any results attributed to that offer and cohort.

## A. Offline fixtures

Use ten synthetic accounts/events: strong fit, weak fit, wrong country, stale signal, uncertain buyer, duplicate branch, unavailable email, reply before send, opt-out surviving reimport and an ambiguous dispatch timeout. Mock all providers and disable sending. Check observable outputs: evidence/unknowns retained, correct fit/exclusion, duplicate handling, suppression, no sending and recoverable saved state. These test the design logic, not a functioning Base44 connection.

## B. Read-only pilot

Thirty accounts in one AU trade category recorded in the campaign. No paid data subscriptions. Use only an existing authorised connection with confirmed quota and a hard no-extra-spend limit, or a manual/supplied-list fallback. Free monthly allowances and trial credits are not guaranteed for every account; do not provision billing, obtain subscriptions or exhaust existing quotas without the required authority. Once access, scope and budget are established, routine read-only work does not need repeated approval.

Review first ten, revise rules, research the remaining twenty, then review all thirty. Collect: total accounts, fit accepted, business phones, published generic/named emails, likely buyer names, channel-eligible contacts, unresolved fields, source usage, actual credits/spend and human/agent time. Report percentages with counts and denominator. Thirty accounts from one category cannot measure coverage in every country.

Measure contact coverage independently from fit. Also track reviewed false positives/negatives and time per accepted account. “No paid data” does not mean no software, model, platform, telephony or staff cost. Do not promise equal performance to paid data without a comparison.

## C. Student rehearsal

In a fresh Base44 student test account, load this package and run ten reviewed records through research, copy, review and CRM handoff. Confirm actual tool availability and writes, reopen the chat and resume, test a missing credential and interrupted job. If using manual research/send steps, test the complete fallback and show where the member acts. Separate authorised team-owned transport tests from prospect outreach.

## D. Live pilot, separately authorised

Before sending: approved cohort, offer/copy/sequence versions, sender identities, channels, current country rules, dates/timezones, limits, reply owner, booking capacity and stop control. Start with the reviewed cohort and a fixed review date. No automatic expansion from weak responses. Log manual actions and confirm any provider-side stops.

Measure accepted/delivered/failed sends, positive/negative replies, opt-outs, conversations, booked/held/qualified calls, proposals, wins, collected cash and total cost. Do not use opens as the primary result. Keep observation windows visible.

## E. Learn and upgrade

Only after the path functions, compare ranked versus otherwise eligible randomly ordered accounts, or lean versus Clay, holding relevant factors comparable. Small samples are exploratory. Record which extra contacts Clay found, whether they were usable, and incremental cost/human time. Use observed bottlenecks to choose the upgrade rather than an unsupported fixed monthly-volume threshold.

Candidate release requires both functional evidence and a student teach-back: why an account fits, what is unknown, who to approach, why the message is relevant, how to pause, and where the next action lives. Do not call the package Base44-tested or student-ready before those tests occur.
