# Jarvis Landing Page
Reference-inspected working draft. Updated 31 October 2026 from Blake's simplification feedback.

Read references/funnel-visual-analysis.md before design. It records the actual source order and visual hierarchy. Source screenshots are in the project reference archive; when available compare them directly. Use the student's approved offer record and genuine assets. Missing proof changes the proof treatment, not the entire funnel into a consultancy brochure.

## Choose the page mode

**VSL Landing Page:** audience callout → outcome headline → one short mechanism line → dominant VSL → “See if you qualify” → progressive qualification form. The video carries the problem, explanation, workflow, objections and detailed offer discussion. Do not repeat its script in long written pain, numbered process, feature, guarantee or responsibility sections underneath.

**Offer Testing Landing Page:** use this exact mode name when testing offers without filming. Audience callout → outcome headline → one or two short sentences saying what gets done and for whom → “See if you qualify” → the same qualification/booking flow. No VSL, video placeholder, storyboard, fake player or oversized image substituted for the missing video. Keep it quick to adapt across distinct offer briefs. Do not claim this text-only mode came from a reference video page; it is Blake's requested testing variant.

Use the same approved promise, identity and qualification logic across a controlled VSL/no-VSL comparison. If testing different offers, identify the offer version and traffic source separately. A submitted form is not a qualified booked call, purchase or proof the offer works.

## Select the reference architecture
- Compact Scale Info approach: small audience callout → dominant centred outcome headline (timeframe/remedy only if justified) → short mechanism line → large VSL → qualification CTA and short friction-reduction line. Minimal navigation and competing actions.
- Goh approach, only when a more extensive proof-led page is requested: centred callout/headline/mechanism → prominent VSL → genuine authority/proof strip → application/booking module → relevant client stories/case evidence. Scale proof volume to what exists; do not default to this longer layout.
State the chosen architecture and why. A split-screen software dashboard hero or numbered features tour is not a faithful substitute unless the user requested that deviation.

Default to the compact Scale Info structure. No landing-page FAQ block by default. The saved references document FAQ videos on Goh's confirmation page, not a reason to add generic landing-page FAQs. Only add extra sections when specifically requested or supported by an inspected reference selected for this page; do not invent them to make the page feel complete.

## Build around the offer
The headline communicates the buyer/outcome, not the tool category. Supporting copy briefly explains the mechanism. All claims, exclusions and any remedy match the offer record. Keep research uncertainty in the internal report. Do not add a long guarantee explanation or “shared accountability” section. If an approved risk reversal is used, keep the promise concise and its material conditions readily accessible; if there is no approved promise, omit it. Removing conditions must not leave a misleading unconditional claim. No invented testimonials, authority logos, numbers or urgency.

## Visual execution
Use a deliberately narrow centred reading column, strong headline hierarchy, substantial 16:9 video, restrained brand accent and one obvious next step. Don't turn every section into identical cards. Choose the student's identity; neither Jarvis navy nor reference lime is mandatory. Responsive headline wrapping, balanced whitespace and a visible mobile CTA matter. Reference screenshots must inform spacing, proportions and sequence, not just colour.
For VSL mode only, a missing video can use a clearly identified review placeholder/storyboard and asset brief; never fake playback. For Offer Testing mode remove the video area entirely. If proof is missing, omit proof blocks rather than adding generic process explanations or borrowed outcomes. A genuine demonstration belongs in the VSL or an explicitly requested demo treatment.

## Form and booking
Choose application then calendar, or direct booking, based on qualification needs. Implement only useful fields tied to fit. Separate application submitted, qualified, slot selected and booking confirmed. Design error/retry, missing booking and mobile states. The next page must reflect the real event. Never claim an actual calendar connection from a local redirect.

## Output and review
Deliver rendered page, copy and asset list plus the reference mapping and canonical offer version. Compare the hero and full section order side by side with the selected source before accepting. Judge: buyer relevance, compelling but supportable promise, VSL dominance, proof appropriateness, CTA clarity, reference fidelity, responsive appearance and working interaction. Record visual and functional results separately. A browser pass alone cannot approve the page. Test one complete example before generating multiple businesses.

## Blake’s marketing preferences — confirmed 22 October 2026
For this programme’s student marketing: do not publish offer prices, deposits, retainers or dollar-valued fee waivers in ads, landing pages, VSLs or the public offer statement. Keep pricing/economics in private sales and planning documents. Lead with the buyer’s actual desired outcome (for example sold jobs and cash collected), then explain the mechanism. Intermediate metrics such as replies or appointments support diagnosis; do not automatically make them the main promise. Do not invent a revenue number to make the headline stronger.
A work-free remedy means continued agreed work without the specified management fee until the agreed result; define the target, measurement, responsibilities and costs behind the plain-language promise. Make material conditions accessible. Do not silently replace that remedy with a published price discount.

Default qualification pattern: “See if you qualify”, one question per screen, progress, required-answer validation, back navigation preserving answers and a clear completion state. Ask fit questions before contact details. Match question count to the decision. Base this on the user-requested Scale Info/Typeform interaction; do not claim exact questionnaire replication unless the embedded questions were inspected. No booking confirmation until an actual booking exists.

## Form and booking refinement — 22 October 2026
For Blake’s funnels, ask fit questions one at a time, then collect full name, email and phone together on one final contact screen. Preserve answers on Back; validate all contact fields. Omit completion-time estimates such as “About 2 minutes”. The next page leads with “Thanks, you qualify. Let’s get you booked in” when qualification means eligibility for the discovery call, then displays the booking calendar. Do not silently claim eligibility for the actual service. If genuine exclusion rules exist, route those separately. Avoid an internal scope-review report as the prospect’s next step. Never claim an appointment exists until calendar confirmation. In previews put calendar-connection status in the review treatment, not the headline. Do not copy reference scarcity, proof or personal contact details.
