# Maintained offer library

Entries need ID, reviewed date, service, buyer/region, recurring problem, installed workflow, provider/deployment examples with inspected links, evidence type, current alternatives, delivery route and open questions.

Separate advertising activity, vendor-reported deployment, buyer-confirmed purchase and measured results. Preserve the period and scope behind performance evidence. Team approval means suitable to teach at the stated evidence level, not automatic proof of performance.

The team's reviewed snapshot is bundled as `offers-scaling-2026-10-30.md`. It covers four service families with advertiser names, active-ad counts, dates and adaptations. Counts and claims are reported by the course agent; this packaging did not repeat the Ad Library searches. Direct advertiser URLs and a raw evidence ledger remain to add. Use the snapshot for choosing a service, with its limits, rather than claiming independent performance verification. Older fictional proposals are demonstrations, not catalogue proof.

Review material claims when used, preserve previous versions and retire stale/unsupported entries with reasons. No quota or hype category is required.
