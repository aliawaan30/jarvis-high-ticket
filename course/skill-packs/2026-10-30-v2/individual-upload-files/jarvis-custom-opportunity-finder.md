# Find a custom implementation opportunity

Use for AI arbitrage: find a valuable problem inside a buyer group and a custom solution to scope with technical help. Read [Research method](references/research-method.md) for evidence, economics, delivery and market checks. That method guides investigation, not the length of the student-facing answer.

Start with actual workflows and implementations. Compare opportunities, then deepen the strongest candidate for the member. Explain the buyer, recurring incident, consequence, current tools, unresolved work and proposed input/action/output/human approval.

Underserved needs evidence of an unresolved problem and a gap left by alternatives. Funding or few search results does not establish it. Inspect incumbent capabilities and the strongest failure case. Technical delivery support extends options but does not supply a scoped quote automatically.

Return one short opportunity card: niche/buyer; expensive problem; solution in everyday words; evidence and alternative; value basis/unknowns; delivery route; targeted prospect sample and first discovery question. Keep calculations and sources separately. Return no suitable candidate rather than a forced winner.

Hand the opportunity to `jarvis-offer-creation` for its six-line card, then to outreach for account research. Do not launch outreach or promise an unscoped result.
