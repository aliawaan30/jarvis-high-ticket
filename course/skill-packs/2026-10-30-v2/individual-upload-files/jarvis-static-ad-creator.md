# Jarvis Static Ad Creator
Recovery candidate, 22 October 2026. Read references/static-visual-analysis.md. It distinguishes viewed examples, Blake's preferences and the still-inaccessible Scale Info ad library. Never claim this is a library of proven winning ads.

## Before design
Read the canonical offer, buyer awareness, actual buyer language, ad destination and available proof. Choose one angle: a costly felt problem, a desired result, an objection, the mechanism, or credible proof. Make the audience recognise themselves. Do not default to vague 'save time with AI' or a generic software slogan.

## Creative directions
Choose contrasting concepts that test a real difference, not five recolours.
- Blake's direct callout/story-post format: plain dominant background, audience address and direct short human message, large readable type, one main idea and next step. Avoid brochure layout, miniature branding rails, process diagrams and decorative CTA buttons dominating the composition. This is a user-requested direction; adapt length to actual readability.
- Presenter/outcome: real authorised presenter or relevant visual plus a concise result/mechanism. The 03:05 reference establishes visual hierarchy, not authority we can borrow.
- Familiar scene/problem: a meaningful workplace/use situation paired with specific consequences. Image must clarify the problem, not arbitrary futuristic robots or luxury props.
- Native text carousel: a strong first card followed by a concise argument, explanation or objection sequence. Original author identity; no fake platform engagement/verification. One idea per card.
- Proof creative: one actual authorised quote/result/screenshot with context. Without proof, choose another format. Do not invent a 'client screenshot'.
AI-generated imagery is a production choice, not an angle. Text/diagrams can be code-rendered; use image-generation or real assets when the selected concept benefits from imagery. Preserve editable sources.

## Copy and production
For each concept provide audience, awareness, angle, exact image copy, primary text, headline, CTA, destination and proof basis. Match the ad's promise to the page and VSL. Use confident truthful copy; keep lab notes and evidence status outside the ad canvas except a discreet preview label in a review-only version. No invented performance or numerical guarantee.
Render the selected treatment, then inspect it at actual feed/phone size. Check one-glance audience/problem, hierarchy, legibility, cropping, visual relevance, specificity and curiosity tied to buying intent. Compare with the chosen reference beside it. Reject a technically valid export if it reads like a dull feature poster.

## Funnel fit and testing
The supplied lesson warns that static-to-VSL-call traffic may need more education. Plan the VSL, pre-call and confirmation assets accordingly. Track qualified calls, attendance and sales alongside CTR/CPC; don't conclude success from cheap clicks. Export separate feed/story compositions rather than stretching. No ad publishing or performance claim is implied by generating an image.

## Blake’s marketing preferences — confirmed 22 October 2026
For this programme’s student marketing: do not publish offer prices, deposits, retainers or dollar-valued fee waivers in ads, landing pages, VSLs or the public offer statement. Keep pricing/economics in private sales and planning documents. Lead with the buyer’s actual desired outcome (for example sold jobs and cash collected), then explain the mechanism. Intermediate metrics such as replies or appointments support diagnosis; do not automatically make them the main promise. Do not invent a revenue number to make the headline stronger.
A work-free remedy means continued agreed work without the specified management fee until the agreed result; define the target, measurement, responsibilities and costs behind the plain-language promise. Make material conditions accessible. Do not silently replace that remedy with a published price discount.
