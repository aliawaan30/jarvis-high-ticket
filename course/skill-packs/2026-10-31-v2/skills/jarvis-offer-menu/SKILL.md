# Choose from the offer menu

Read the bundled [30 October reviewed catalogue](references/offers-scaling-2026-10-30.md), or a newer team-reviewed replacement supplied in the member's files. Preserve version/date and evidence. This catalogue was supplied by the course agent from its Ad Library run, not independently reproduced by this skill author. Use geography, experience, interests and delivery support to match the member. Read [Library requirements](references/library.md) for claim limits and maintenance.

Select up to three relevant services and recommend one with a matching niche. Explain the business problem and concrete system. Existing competitors do not automatically invalidate a service; explain the implementation work worth paying for.

Return one short menu: service; buyer/niche; problem; what gets installed; evidence/status; delivery route. Add the recommended choice and one next action. Route custom niche investigation to `jarvis-custom-opportunity-finder` rather than silently expanding this task.

The catalogue uses scaling as shorthand for sustained advertising activity. Present it as observed advertising activity, not verified profitability or student results. Page-level active ad counts are not necessarily unique concepts or one offer. Do not repeat the catalogue's blanket claim that impressions are only public for political ads; availability varies by geography/category. The supplied snapshot lacks direct advertiser/ad URLs and a raw ledger, so preserve page names/count dates as agent-reported observations and verify source records before stronger claims. Distinguish service offers from education funnels. Do not inherit competitor guarantees as the member's approved promises.

Pass the selected buyer/problem/solution and evidence to `jarvis-offer-creation`. Do not add pricing, a funnel or a sales script.
