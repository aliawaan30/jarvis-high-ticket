# The lean outreach path (v2.1, 29 Oct 2026)

Blake asked for a simpler, cheaper way to run the lab and to make sure members can find leads properly. This replaces the Clay-first lab in SYSTEM.md section 6 phases A and B. Clay stays in the design as the upgrade when a member is scaling past a few hundred accounts a month. Nothing here launches outreach; design and a read-only pilot only.

## The call

Run the lab on Blake's own Jarvis for business owners offer, with zero paid data. Every tool below is free at lab and member volume.

## What a member does, in order

1. **Tell Jarvis the account.** Industry, country, city or region, size, and the pain the offer fixes (from Module 2).
2. **Jarvis builds the list from Google Places.** The free course Lead Finder already does this: name, phone, address, website, hours, rating, review count. Google gives US$300 credit plus a free monthly tier, so a member list of 300 accounts costs nothing. Works in every country Google Maps covers (US, AU, UK, CA, NZ, EU).
3. **Jarvis reads each website.** It fetches the site and the contact page and pulls the owner name, email, booking method, services, staff size, and the pain signals (no online booking, no after hours answer, job ads, old site). This replaces Clay enrichment for local businesses, where the email is on the site most of the time.
4. **Jarvis finds the owner from the free registry.** AU: ABN Lookup (free API). UK: Companies House (free API). NZ: Companies Office. US: state Secretary of State search, varies by state. Canada: provincial registries. Where no registry gives a name, LinkedIn by hand for the top accounts only.
5. **Jarvis scores and ranks.** Pain signals plus size plus reachability. The member reviews the top 30 by hand.
6. **Jarvis writes the sequence.** Call script, email, LinkedIn note, DM, one angle adapted per channel, per the country rules in SYSTEM.md section 3.
7. **The member runs it.** Humans dial the listed business number. Email from their own Google Workspace through the CRM. LinkedIn and DMs by hand. Replies and opt outs stop the sequence everywhere.

What they get at the end: a ranked list of real local businesses with phone, email where the site has it, owner name where a registry has it, a pain reason per account, and a written sequence, inside their CRM, ready to run. Cost: nothing beyond their time.

## Where this is weaker than Clay, honestly

- Email coverage. Sites list an email for roughly half of local businesses. Clay's waterfall finds more, especially for companies with staff and no public email. For trades, clinics and local services the phone is the main channel anyway, so the gap matters less.
- Mobile numbers. Neither path gives these reliably. Humans call the listed number.
- Speed at volume. Past a few hundred accounts a month, Clay is faster. Teach it as the upgrade in the last outreach lesson.

## Lab, lean version

- **A. Offline rehearsal.** Unchanged, ten fixtures, outbound disabled.
- **B. Read-only pilot, zero spend.** 30 real accounts in one AU trade category, Blake's offer, Places plus site reads plus ABN Lookup. Measure: accounts with phone, with email, with owner name, pain signal precision on the first ten labelled by hand, minutes of review per account. No messages sent.
- **C, D, E.** Unchanged from SYSTEM.md.

## What this unblocks

Codex can build the coordinator skill and the list, enrich and score references against free tools now. The Clay adapter becomes an optional reference. Blake no longer has to open a Clay account or authorise spend for the lab.
