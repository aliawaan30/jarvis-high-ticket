Our story

Since [2014], coffee that starts conversations.

Copperline began with one second-hand roaster in a Deep Ellum garage. Today we roast small batches every week for the café, the neighbourhood and a few hundred doorsteps across Dallas.

The journey

The journey of a bean

Where it starts.

We buy from a small number of farms we know by name, and we pay for quality, not volume.

The roast.

Twelve kilo batches, roasted by ear and by numbers, so every bag tastes like the last.

The rest.

Fresh coffee needs a few days to settle. We ship it when it's ready, never before.
