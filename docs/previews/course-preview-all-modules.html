How Jarvis 2.0 Works

Jarvis 2.0 doesn't wait for you to ask. It runs your agency with you, every day, toward one number.

1. Four parts that work together

Know what's running, so you know what to expect from it.
The brainHow Jarvis thinks. It looks at your business, works out what makes money soonest, and does that first. Sales calls beat outreach, every time.
The rhythmWhat Jarvis does on its own. A brief every morning, a log every evening, your numbers every Friday, and it watches your replies all day.
The missionsYour path to your first client, in 19 steps. One at a time. Each one only counts when the proof is in your HUD.
The HUDYour agency on one screen. Leads, calls, campaigns, clients and your missions. You can talk to Jarvis from it too.

2. Your 19 missions

You always know exactly what's next, and how far you've come.
12345678910111213141516171819
Set upOffer and funnelOutreachSalesDelivery

Mission 1 is Jarvis 2.0 going live. Mission 14 is your first booked call. Mission 17 is your first client. Mission 19 is that client live.

3. How every Jarvis lesson works

Learn it once. Every module works the same way.

Each module starts with a file. You attach it and Jarvis installs that module's skills. Then every lesson gives you one message to send, names the skill it uses, and tells you what you'll see when it worked. If Jarvis says an earlier step comes first, it's right. Do that step and it tells you when to come back.

When a skill gets better, a lesson tells you. Attach the new file and send:

Update these skills. Keep all my data. Tell me which versions changed.

And whenever you're not sure what's next:

Where am I?

4. Why this is different

Most AI tools wait for you to know what to ask. Jarvis knows where you are, what's next, and what's making money, and it tells you before you ask. It checks its own work, it never makes up a number, and when something breaks it fixes it with you instead of giving you an error.

Your Jarvis HUD

Your HUD is your agency on one screen: dashboard, pipeline, leads, campaigns, clients, and Talk to Jarvis. About 20 minutes.

1. Start it in Jarvis

Jarvis walks you through every step and checks each one before the next.
Use the jarvis-hq skill to set up my Agency HQ.

You: Use the jarvis-hq skill to set up my Agency HQ.

Jarvis: Let's do it. First, in Base44 click Create new app and name it Agency HQ. Don't type anything into it yet. Tell me when it's there.

2. Install the app

This message goes into the new app's builder chat, not into Jarvis. That's the one mistake that costs people an evening.
  1. Open your new Agency HQ app in Base44.
  2. In its builder chat, attach the file from this lesson.
  3. Paste the message below in the same message and send it.
agency-hq-v3.14.zip
Install the attached Agency HQ v3.14 package into this app as the exact source of truth. Unzip it and copy every file under source/ into this project at the same paths, replacing what is there. Make sure every entity exists exactly as defined (18 tables). Deploy every backend function exactly as written (19 functions). Register the workflow and confirm it is active. Do not redesign, simplify or recreate anything. Run the build and fix only genuine build errors. Report every table, every deployed function with its status code, the workflow status and the build result. Do not publish.
Before you carry on: send Jarvis a screenshot of the builder's report. If anything's missing, Jarvis gives you the exact message to send the builder.

3. Add your two keys

Keys let your HUD and Jarvis talk to each other safely. Two are needed. They go in the app, never in a chat.
KeyWhat it's forWhere it comes from
HUD keyLets your HUD prove it's really you when Jarvis or a voice call writes to it.Jarvis makes it for you and shows it once. Copy it.
Jarvis access tokenLets the HUD hand things to Jarvis, like a task from a voice call.You make it in Jarvis. Steps below.

Make the Jarvis access token:

  1. Open your Jarvis agent's settings, then Developer.
  2. Click Create access token.
  3. Choose One app or Superagent, pick the entry with the Agent badge, and choose Full access.
  4. Copy the token.

Add both keys to the app:

  1. Open your Agency HQ app, then Dashboard, then Secrets.
  2. Add HUD_KEY with the HUD key, and JARVIS_NATIVE_TOKEN with the access token. The names must be exactly these.
  3. Never paste a key into Jarvis or any chat. If you did by accident, make a new one.

4. Publish twice, then connect

Keys only start working once the app is published. Publishing twice makes sure they've loaded.
  1. Click Publish. When it's done, click Publish again.
  2. Open your published HUD, then Settings. Add your Jarvis agent ID and your name. For voice, choose Base44. You can switch to Retell later.
  3. In Jarvis's settings, General, give Jarvis access to the Agency HQ app.
  4. Tell Jarvis "Done". It runs a check on every part.
Before you carry on: your HUD opens with your missions on the dashboard, and Jarvis says your HQ is ready.

If it looks different: tell Jarvis what you see, or send a screenshot. It knows this step and will sort it out with you.

Create Your Offer

The most important lesson in the course. Your offer is what every page, email and call is built on. Jarvis writes it with you in six lines.

1. What your offer is

An owner should get it in ten seconds and want to know more.

Your offer is six lines. If a roofer can't repeat it back after one read, nothing you build after it will land. Here's each line, with the Dallas roofer:

LineWhat it saysRoofer example
NicheExactly who it's for. Narrow wins.Residential roofers in Dallas doing $100k+ a month
OutcomeThe result they care about, in their words.5 more signed jobs from estimates you've already sent
TimeframeHow fast. A real number.In 30 days
GuaranteeWhat happens if it doesn't work.Or we keep working free until you do
ProofWhy they should trust you. Real only.A working demo built on your own estimates
MechanismHow it works, in one line.Every unsold estimate followed up within 24 hours

2. Pick your guarantee

This is the line that makes the offer easy to say yes to. Only you can choose it.
GuaranteeWhen to use it
Work free until the resultStrongest. Use it when you're confident you can deliver.
Money back on the setup feeStrong and simple. Good for a first client.
Pay the second half at go liveLower risk for you, still easy for them.
No guaranteeFine, but your offer needs stronger proof.

Whatever you pick only covers your fee, never their ad spend or tool costs. And you decide which size of business gets it, so you only promise results to owners you know you can deliver for.

3. Write it with Jarvis

Jarvis already knows your niche and service, so you don't paste anything.
Use the offer creation skill to write my offer.

You: Use the offer creation skill to write my offer.

Jarvis: Three things only you can decide. Which guarantee are you comfortable with? How many hours a week can you give clients? And which size of roofer gets the guarantee? I'd suggest $100k or more a month.

You: Work free until the result. 10 hours a week. $100k plus.

Jarvis: Residential roofers in Dallas doing $100k+ a month, old quote recovery, work free until the result, 10 hours a week. Ready for me to write it?

You: Yes.

Jarvis: Here it is. Approve it, or tell me what to change.

NicheResidential roofers in Dallas doing $100k+ a month
Outcome5 more signed jobs from the estimates you've already sent
TimeframeIn 30 days
GuaranteeOr we keep working free until you do. Covers our fee, not your ad costs
ProofA working demo built on your own estimates
MechanismEvery unsold estimate gets followed up within 24 hours
Before you carry on: you can say your offer out loud in one breath, and you'd be happy to read it to a roofer.

4. Change it, then approve it

Change anything, as many times as you like. Then lock it in.

Tell Jarvis what to change in plain words, like "make it 45 days" or "use money back instead". It rewrites only that line. When you're happy:

Done when: open your HUD. Your offer shows as approved with today's date, and your mission moves on.

Your first campaign, set up right. You click, Jarvis tells you what to click.
