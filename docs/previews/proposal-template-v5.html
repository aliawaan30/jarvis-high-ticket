Missed call and job intake system

Coastline misses after hours calls and re-types every job into Jobber by hand. We will install an AI receptionist that answers those calls, books suitable jobs and creates them in Jobber, with a short summary for you each morning.

The problemand its impact
28
new customer calls missed a month

Calls after 5pm go to voicemail. Most of those customers ring the next plumber.

12 hours
a week re-typing call notes

Every enquiry is copied into Jobber by hand, so quotes go out a day late.

28 calls x 20% that become jobs x $1,400 average job = about $94,000 a year in potential work. Office time: 12 hours x $32 an hour = about $20,000 a year, time freed up rather than cash saved. Figures from our call on 6 November.

Proposed build
  1. 1

    After hours receptionist. Answers missed and after hours calls on your number, takes the details and books suitable jobs.

  2. 2

    Job intake. Every booking becomes a Jobber job with the caller's details, so nobody re-types notes.

  3. 3

    Morning summary. One message at 7am with new jobs, urgent calls and anything that needs a person.

Connects to your phone line, Google Calendar and Jobber. People still decide quotes and emergency call outs. Not included website, ads or quoting.
