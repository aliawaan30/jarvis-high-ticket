# Decisions, 18 November 2026: premium websites

1. Website delivery is three skills in Module 7: website research, brand guide, premium website (v4 of Blake's premium website builder v3.0.0).
2. Research comes first and decides every section: customer, rules, market, search, client facts. Rule packs are starting points, always re-checked and dated.
3. Every client gets three brand directions (palette with checked contrast, type from a licensed library, voice, photography and film, motion). The member picks or mixes; it becomes the brand guideline.
4. The five v3 sites stay as the quality bar and test examples, not templates. Sites are composed from a 30-pattern section library.
5. Higgsfield is the media route (API, the member's own prepaid account, keys saved as Jarvis secrets). Cost shown and approved before any spend.
6. Client delivery is part of the skill: real enquiry destination, SEO basics, privacy page, launch checks, the client's own domain, handover.
7. Regulated niches get a sign-off gate before launch (for example a Texas med spa's medical director).
8. One starting prompt runs the whole website flow: a seven-question intake (client, style, contact details, enquiry email, services, existing assets, language), research and brand run automatically, then three check-ins only: the plan and cost, the review, launch.
9. Higgsfield: first-time connection inside the flow, a look line and shot recipe for every shot, films made from approved stills, drafts first, all shots submitted together, cost and time shown first, and plain-words errors (out of credit, key rejected, blocked prompt, busy). Tested live: Jarvis generated films through the API on 18 November.
10. Strict design system: tokens and shared components before pages; surfaces (light, dark, image) decide every component's colours automatically; three button variants; custom form controls; overlays only behind text; no gap under the header; the sticky bar never covers content; layout QA at 360, 390, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440 px.
11. Phones: every image has a phone version, no empty blocks, a phone menu, swipe carousels, the hero film plays as a muted loop with its still as the fallback.
12. Image direction: roles and shapes planned before generating, a variety check so no two sections repeat, every image fits its section's meaning, niche design profiles decide what fits.
13. Every brand direction includes a starter logo drawn as SVG.
14. The website owns its enquiries: one pipeline for every form, saved in its own table, an alert to the client and a confirmation to the visitor, a copy to Jarvis only server to server with a secret key, no open endpoints.
15. Copy is written natively in the site's language and formality, never translated; a native speaker review is recommended before launch.
